中國的「宜居」國家戰略

規模效應 · 財政紓困 · 重建消費力
GFI · 2026年9月

「宜居」不是口號。它是中國經濟轉型的結構性支柱——透過降低居住成本，釋放消費潛力，重建財政可持續性。

一、為什麼「宜居」是國家戰略？

在西方，住房被視為金融資產。在中國，住房被視為民生基礎。這個根本差異決定了政策走向。

當住房成本佔據家庭收入 50% 以上時，消費被壓抑，生育率下降，社會流動性凍結。中國的「宜居」戰略正是為了解決這個結構性問題。

二、三大支柱

1. 規模效應

中國每年新建住房超過 1000 萬套，透過大規模供應壓低價格。這不是市場調節——這是制度性供應管理。

2. 財政紓困

降低土地出讓金依賴，轉向房地產稅和消費稅。地方政府從「賣地財政」轉向「服務財政」。

3. 重建消費力

當住房成本下降，家庭可支配收入增加。這不是「刺激消費」——這是釋放被住房擠壓的消費潛力。

中國的「宜居」戰略不是福利政策。它是結構性改革——透過降低居住成本，釋放消費潛力，重建財政可持續性。這是一套完整的經濟轉型路徑。

China's 'Affordable Living' National Strategy

Scale Effects · Fiscal Relief · Rebuilding Spending Power
GFI · September 2026

"Affordable Living" is not a slogan. It is the structural pillar of China's economic transformation — reducing housing costs to unlock consumption potential and rebuild fiscal sustainability.

I. Why Is "Affordable Living" a National Strategy?

In the West, housing is treated as a financial asset. In China, housing is treated as a livelihood foundation. This fundamental difference determines policy direction.

When housing costs consume over 50% of household income, consumption is suppressed, fertility rates fall, and social mobility freezes. China's "Affordable Living" strategy is designed to solve this structural problem.

II. Three Pillars

1. Scale Effects

China builds over 10 million new housing units annually, suppressing prices through large-scale supply. This is not market adjustment — it is institutional supply management.

2. Fiscal Relief

Reducing dependence on land transfer fees, shifting toward property taxes and consumption taxes. Local governments move from "land sales finance" to "service finance."

3. Rebuilding Spending Power

When housing costs fall, household disposable income increases. This is not "stimulating consumption" — it is unlocking consumption potential that was squeezed by housing.

China's "Affordable Living" strategy is not a welfare policy. It is structural reform — reducing housing costs to unlock consumption potential and rebuild fiscal sustainability. This is a complete economic transformation path.
