被聯合國頒獎的理想，
被中國建造的現實

維納斯計劃、土地制度與西方地主階級的恐懼
2026年8月 · GFI Flow Intelligence · 閱讀約10分鐘

2016年7月，聯合國NOVUS峰會上，一個終身被西方主流社會視為「科幻迷」的老人，以視訊連線的方式出現在大螢幕上。

雅克·弗斯科（Jacque Fresco），維納斯計劃（The Venus Project）創始人，因其「城市設計與社區」理念，獲得聯合國頒發的「可持續城市與社區」獎項。他當時已太虛弱，無法親自出席。隔年，2017年，他辭世了。

這是一個殘酷的隱喻：他構想的世界被認證了，但他已經沒有力氣去建造它了。

西方主流媒體幾乎未報導此事，更未在他辭世時給予應有的版面——不是因為沒看見，而是因為無法面對一個事實：他們剛剛為一個嘲笑了一輩子的「烏托邦」頒了獎。

與此同時，在太平洋的另一端，一個被西方同樣嘲笑的國家，正在把那個「烏托邦」拆解成可執行的制度，鋪在960萬平方公里的土地上——沒有宣言，沒有發布會，只是繼續修路、通電、通網、脫貧。

聯合國頒獎的理想，中國正在建造。

要理解西方為什麼必須嘲笑這一切，就要先理解西方制度的底層邏輯。

一、維納斯計劃：被聯合國頒獎、被西方忽視

維納斯計劃的核心主張，可概括為四個層面：

第一，資源系統管理。地球資源是全人類的共同遺產，不應被私人資本捕獲。資源應通過科學方法進行系統性管理，而非交由市場盲目分配。

第二，消除稀缺。稀缺不是必然的，而是分配機制失能的產物。只要科學管理資源、消除浪費、實現高效配置，人類完全可以實現普遍富足。

第三，超越貨幣中介。在高度自動化的社會中，資源分配不再依賴貨幣體系，而是基於科學計算和實際需求。貨幣的統治力將被公共品供給所稀釋。

第四，系統工程思維。社會問題不是孤立的——交通、能源、住房、教育、醫療彼此嵌套，必須在全局框架下求解，而非頭痛醫頭、腳痛醫腳。

這套思想體系在西方遭遇了系統性的邊緣化：學術界將其歸入「未來學」或「科幻研究」；媒體定位為「烏托邦社區」或「邊緣思想」；公眾則理解為「佛羅里達一個怪人建的實驗基地」。直到2016年聯合國頒獎，維納斯計劃才獲得一次「體制內認證」，但西方主流選擇假裝沒看見。

為什麼？因為維納斯計劃最核心的主張——「地球資源是全人類的共同遺產，不應被私人資本捕獲」——直接刺穿了西方制度的命門。

二、西方制度的底色：不是「民主」，是「地主」

西方自詡「民主燈塔」，但如果撕開「自由民主」的包裝紙，露出來的是一張地主階級的現代面孔。

（一）英國：0.6%的人掌握三分之二的土地

  • 僅占人口0.6%的世襲貴族和鄉紳，掌握著全國約三分之二的土地
  • 英格蘭一半的土地，掌握在不足1%的人口手中
  • 英國上議院至今保留著92個世襲貴族席位——無需選舉、不向任何選民負責，僅憑血統參與制定影響6600萬人生活的法律
  • 第七代西敏公爵休·格羅夫納，在倫敦梅菲爾和貝爾格萊維亞區擁有約300英畝土地，估值60億英鎊——他不需要管理、不需要創新、不需要服務任何人，僅憑「合法」繼承權，就擁有比伯明罕市全年財政預算還多的財富

一個讓血統決定立法的國家，談什麼「民主」？

一個讓0.6%的人掌握三分之二土地的國家，談什麼「公平」？

（二）美國：沒有世襲貴族，但有資本貴族

  • 2024年美國大選花費159億美元，排名前1%的捐贈者提供了70%以上的「超級政治行動委員會」資金
  • 「旋轉門」是制度化常態：高盛高管當財政部長，石油公司前CEO掌內政部，製藥公司高管制定醫保政策
  • 收入最低的50%家庭僅擁有全國2.5%的財富，而最高的1%擁有32%以上
  • 約2600萬人沒有醫保，每年約4萬人死於槍擊——幾十年無法通過控槍法案，不是因為「民主」失靈，是因為資本不想讓它通過

政府不是為人民服務的，是為資本地主管家的。

三、維納斯計劃：刺穿地主命門的一把刀

現在我們可以理解：西方為什麼必須嘲笑維納斯計劃？因為它的核心主張——「地球資源是全人類的共同遺產，不應被私人資本捕獲」——直接否定了西方地主階級的存在基礎。

如果資源真的是全人類的，那英國貴族的2000萬英畝土地算什麼？美國1%的人口掌握的32%財富算什麼？世襲的立法席位算什麼？資本對政策的系統性操控算什麼？

他們嘲笑維納斯，不是因為「不切實際」，而是因為維納斯否定了他們「切」了數百年的「實際」。

所以聯合國頒獎，他們假裝沒看見；雅克辭世，他們假裝沒發生；維納斯計劃，他們繼續定義為「科幻」。因為一旦承認維納斯有價值，就等於承認自己的制度有問題。

四、中國：被嘲笑，因為正在建造被頒獎的理想

中國做的事情——土地公有制、土地增值回饋公共財政、8億人脫貧、大規模公共品供給、連續70年系統規劃——正是維納斯「資源公共管理」構想在大規模實踐中最接近的版本。

維納斯計劃（被聯合國頒獎） 中國實踐（被西方嘲笑）
資源應科學管理、統一調配「五年規劃」連續70年，全國一盤棋
稀缺可通過系統設計消除8億人脫貧，人類最大規模減貧

貨幣不應是唯一分配手段「兩不愁三保障」，公共品替代貨幣購買力
長周期規劃、避免短期逐利跨世紀工程：南水北調、西電東送
系統工程思維替代碎片化治理統籌兼顧、整體施策、多措並舉

同一種系統性治理邏輯，在維納斯計劃中被聯合國頒獎，在中國實踐中被西方嘲笑——這不是認知差異，是雙重標準。

五、雙重標準：嘲笑雅克，也嘲笑中國

這形成了西方精英的雙重嘲笑：

第一重：嘲笑雅克。「資源導向型經濟？超越貨幣？系統管理一切？太天真了！」

第二重：嘲笑中國。「中國搞什麼『五年規劃』？高鐵建那麼多虧本怎麼辦？土地公有？那不是剝奪私人產權嗎？」

問題是——中國正在證明雅克是對的。

他們嘲笑雅克，因為他構想了一個他們不敢建造的未來；他們嘲笑中國，因為她正在建造那個未來。聯合國頒獎的構想，中國正在執行。嘲笑雅克，等於嘲笑他們自己不敢面對的極限；嘲笑中國，等於嘲笑他們自己不敢承認的失敗。

而中國，沒有停下來解釋——只是繼續修路、通電、通網、脫貧，用現實回答嘲笑。

六、盎格魯-薩克遜的「規則陷阱」：媒體、軍事與「民主」輸出

在西方制度的底層，存在一個比「民主」更深層的驅動力——盎格魯-薩克遜精英階級試圖掌控全球的企圖。

這群人約占全球人口的12%，卻掌控了主流媒體的敘事權、國際金融的規則制定權、以及全球軍事基地的部署權。他們透過一套綿密而持久的機制，試圖支配其餘的88%人口：

  • 媒體操控。六大集團控制全球90%以上的主流媒體。他們的編輯室決定什麼是「新聞」、什麼是「價值」、什麼是「正義」。他們將「民主」包裝成普世價值，卻從不報導英國上議院的世襲席位、美國資本對政策的操控、土地增值被私人捕獲的真相。
  • 軍事佔領。美國在全球80個國家擁有約800個軍事基地，占全球外國軍事基地總數的70%。他們以「民主推廣」為名發動戰爭，卻在占領區扶植聽命於華盛頓的傀儡政權。從阿富汗到伊拉克，從利比亞到敘利亞——軍事佔領從未帶來「民主」，只帶來了廢墟、難民和資源掠奪。
  • 價值殖民。他們透過好萊塢電影、華爾街金融、矽谷技術，將「私有產權神聖化」、「市場萬能論」灌輸給全世界。任何挑戰這套價值體系的國家——無論是嘗試土地公有制的中國，還是倡導資源公共管理的維納斯計劃——都會被貼上「威權」、「專制」、「烏托邦」的標籤。

他們發動軍事佔領，不是為了傳播「民主」——而是為了確保他們的規則成為唯一的規則。

12%的盎格魯-薩克遜精英，試圖透過媒體和軍事，掌控88%的其餘人口。

他們嘲笑的，不是維納斯的「不切實際」——而是維納斯對他們「規則」的根本質疑。

他們嘲笑的，不是中國的「落後」——而是中國正在建造一個他們規則之外的世界。

七、結語：維納斯在西方是「幻想」，在中國是「現實」

雅克·弗斯科用一生畫了一張藍圖。聯合國在他生命最後時刻蓋了章。而中國，正在把那張藍圖變成現實。

這不是「中國採納了維納斯計劃」——中國從未在政策文件中寫過這句話。但如果將維納斯的核心理念與中國過去幾十年的實踐對照，會發現一種方法論的延續性：跨區域資源調配（南水北調、西電東送）、大規模公共品供給（高鐵、電網、通信、醫療、教育）、土地增值公共回饋、連續70年系統規劃——維納斯的核心主張，在中國都能找到對應的制度實踐。

維納斯在西方是一個「幻想」。

維納斯在中國，變成了「現實」。

12%的盎格魯-薩克遜精英繼續制定規則，透過媒體和軍事控制世界。88%的其餘人口繼續建造現實——而8億人脫貧的真相，比任何「民主」修辭都更沉重。

英國的土地歸貴族，中國的土地歸人民。

誰在真正治理？誰在替地主管家？

答案，不言自明。

The Ideal Honored by the UN,
Built by China

The Venus Project, Land Institutions, and the Fear of the Western Landed Class
August 2026 · GFI Flow Intelligence · ~10 min read

In July 2016, at the UN NOVUS Summit, an elderly man—dismissed by Western mainstream society as a "science fiction enthusiast" for his entire life—appeared on screen via video link.

Jacque Fresco, founder of The Venus Project, received the "Sustainable Cities and Communities" award. He was already too frail to attend in person. The following year, in 2017, he passed away.

This is a brutal metaphor: his vision was certified, but he no longer had the strength to build it.

Western mainstream media barely reported the event, nor did they give him proper coverage when he died—not because they didn't see it, but because they could not face the fact: they had just awarded the very "utopia" they had mocked for a lifetime.

Meanwhile, on the other side of the Pacific, a country equally mocked by the West was dismantling that "utopia" and transforming it into executable institutions—across 9.6 million square kilometers. No manifestos, no press conferences—just roads, electricity, internet, and poverty alleviation.

The ideal honored by the UN—China is building it.

To understand why the West must mock all of this, we must first understand the underlying logic of Western institutions.

I. The Venus Project: Honored by the UN, Ignored by the West

The core propositions of The Venus Project can be summarized in four dimensions: systematic resource management (Earth's resources are the common heritage of all humanity, not to be captured by private capital); elimination of scarcity (scarcity is not inevitable—it is the product of dysfunctional distribution); transcending monetary intermediation (in a highly automated society, resource distribution relies on scientific calculation rather than money); and systems engineering thinking (social problems are nested and must be solved within a global framework).

This body of thought was systematically marginalized by Western mainstream society—academia relegated it to "futurology" or "science fiction studies"; media framed it as a "utopian community" or "fringe idea"; the public understood it as "a strange experimental base built by an oddball in Florida." It wasn't until the 2016 UN award that The Venus Project received any form of institutional recognition—but that recognition was met with willful ignorance by the Western mainstream.

Why? Because the central proposition of The Venus Project—"Earth's resources are the common heritage of all humanity and should not be captured by private capital"—strikes directly at the heart of the Western system.

II. The True Nature of Western Institutions: Not "Democracy," but "Landlordism"

The West calls itself a "beacon of democracy." But if you tear away the wrapping paper of "liberal democracy," what emerges is the modern face of the landlord class.

(I) The UK: 0.6% of the Population Controls Two-Thirds of the Land

  • Only 0.6% of the population—hereditary aristocrats and gentry—controls about two-thirds of the country's land
  • Half of England's land is held by less than 1% of the population
  • The UK House of Lords still reserves 92 hereditary seats—members who need no election, answer to no voters, and by blood alone participate in legislation affecting 66 million people
  • The 7th Duke of Westminster, Hugh Grosvenor, owns approximately 300 acres in London's Mayfair and Belgravia, valued at £6 billion—he manages nothing, innovates nothing, serves no one, and simply by "legal" inheritance possesses more wealth than Birmingham's annual budget

A country where blood determines legislation—what "democracy"?

A country where 0.6% controls two-thirds of the land—what "fairness"?

(II) The U.S.: No Hereditary Aristocracy, but a Capitalist Aristocracy

  • The 2024 U.S. election cost $15.9 billion, with the top 1% of donors providing over 70% of Super PAC funding
  • The "revolving door" is institutionalized: Goldman Sachs executives become Treasury Secretaries; former oil company CEOs run the Interior Department; pharmaceutical executives shape healthcare policy
  • The lowest-income 50% of households hold only 2.5% of national wealth, while the top 1% holds over 32%
  • ~26 million have no health insurance, ~40,000 die from gun violence annually, and gun control legislation has been stalled for decades—not because "democracy" is broken, but because capital doesn't want it to pass

Government does not serve the people—it manages assets for the capitalist-landlord class.

III. The Venus Project: A Blade Piercing the Landlord Class

Now we can understand: why must the West mock The Venus Project? Because its central proposition—"Earth's resources are the common heritage of all humanity and should not be captured by private capital"—directly negates the very foundation of the Western landlord class.

If resources truly belong to all of humanity, then what are the 20 million acres of British aristocrats? The 32% of wealth held by the top 1% in America? Hereditary legislative seats? Capital's systematic control over policy?

They mock Venus not because it is "impractical"—but because it negates the "practical" they have been extracting for centuries.

So when the UN gave the award, they pretended not to see it; when Jacque passed away, they pretended it didn't happen; and The Venus Project? They continue to label it "science fiction." Because once you admit Venus has value, you admit your own system has problems.

What China has done—public land ownership, land value appreciation returned to public finance, 800 million people lifted out of poverty, large-scale public goods provision, 70 years of systematic planning—is the closest real-world approximation of Venus's "resource commons management" vision on a massive scale.

V. Double Standards: Mocking Jacque, Mocking China

This creates the Western elite's double mockery: first, mocking Jacque—"Resource-based economy? Beyond money? Systematically manage everything? Too naive!" Second, mocking China—"What is China doing with 'Five-Year Plans'? Building high-speed rail at a loss? Public land ownership? Isn't that depriving private property rights?"

But China is proving Jacque right.

They mock Jacque because he envisioned a future they dare not build; they mock China because she is building that future. The ideal honored by the UN is being implemented in China. Mocking Jacque is mocking the limits they dare not face; mocking China is mocking the failure they dare not admit.

And China does not stop to explain—China just keeps building roads, power lines, internet, and lifting people out of poverty, answering mockery with reality.

Beneath the surface of Western institutions lies a force deeper than "democracy": the Anglo-Saxon elite's ambition to dominate the globe.

12% of Anglo-Saxon elites, through media and military, seek to control the remaining 88%.

They mock not Venus's "impracticality"—but its fundamental challenge to their rules.

They mock not China's "backwardness"—but that China is building a world beyond their rules.

VII. Conclusion: Venus Is a "Fantasy" in the West, "Reality" in China

Jacque Fresco spent a lifetime drawing a blueprint. The UN stamped that blueprint at the very end of his life. And China is turning that blueprint into reality.

This is not "China adopted The Venus Project"—China never wrote that in any policy document. But if you compare Venus's core principles with China's practice over the past decades, you will find a methodological continuity: cross-regional resource allocation (South-North Water Transfer, West-East Power Transmission); large-scale public goods provision (high-speed rail, power grids, communications, healthcare, education); land value appreciation returned to the public; 70 years of continuous systematic planning—Venus's core principles all find their corresponding institutional practice in China.

Venus is a "fantasy" in the West.

The 12% Anglo-Saxon elite continues to make the rules, controlling the world through media and military might. The 88% continues to build reality—and the truth of 800 million people lifted out of poverty is heavier than any "democracy" rhetoric.

Britain's land belongs to the aristocracy. China's land belongs to the people.

Who is truly governing? Who is managing assets for the landlord class?

The answer speaks for itself.
