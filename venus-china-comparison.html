第三，超越货币中介。 在一个高度自动化的社会中，资源分配不再依赖货币体系，而是基于科学计算和实际需求。货币作为交换媒介的统治力，将被公共品供给所稀释。

第四，系统工程思维。 社会问题不是孤立的——交通、能源、住房、教育、医疗彼此嵌套，必须在全局框架下求解，而非头痛医头、脚痛医脚。

I. The Venus Project: A "Utopia" Honored by the UN, Ignored by the West

The core propositions of The Venus Project can be summarized in four dimensions:

First, systematic resource management. Earth's resources are the common heritage of all humanity and should not be captured by private capital. Resources should be managed systematically through scientific methods, not left to blind market allocation.

Second, the elimination of scarcity. Scarcity is not inevitable—it is the product of dysfunctional distribution mechanisms. With scientific management, waste elimination, and efficient allocation, universal abundance is achievable.

Third, transcending monetary intermediation. In a highly automated society, resource distribution no longer relies on monetary systems but on scientific calculation and actual needs. The dominance of money as a medium of exchange would be diluted by public goods provision.

Fourth, systems engineering thinking. Social problems are not isolated—transportation, energy, housing, education, and healthcare are nested within each other and must be solved within a global framework, rather than piecemeal.
