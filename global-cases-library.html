GL-US-001 — United States: Public Service Loan Forgiveness (PSLF)

Period: 2007–present | Domain: Higher Education / Debt Relief
GL Score: 0.006 (Critical Risk)

Citizen Objective: A teacher, nurse, or public servant who worked 10 years in qualifying employment making 120 qualifying payments, attempting to receive promised loan forgiveness.

Friction Findings: Document complexity (5/5), annual recertification (5/5), retroactive rule changes (5/5), information black hole (4/5), legal language complexity (4/5).

GL = (0.30 × 9.2) / (100 × 4.5) = 0.006

Core Insight: A program's legitimacy cannot be measured by stated generosity. When success rate is under 1%, the program has mathematically failed.
