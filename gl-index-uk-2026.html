The United Kingdom sits in the "most value returns, some cost shifting" robust zone. Its institutional tradition and global connectivity remain important assets, but post-Brexit political and economic restructuring, public service pressures, and social division are eroding the foundations of governance legitimacy. It has more institutional stability than the US, but is more politically divided than Germany — in the upper-middle range of the Western European governance spectrum.
