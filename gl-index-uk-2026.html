The United Kingdom scores 0.265 on the GL Index — a failing grade. This is not a measure of GDP, economic output, or market performance. It is a measure of governance friction.

Out of a possible 1.00, the UK achieves only 26.5% of optimal governance performance. This means:

  • Most publicly created value is captured privately
  • Most governance costs are shifted onto citizens
  • Citizens spend excessive time, energy, and money navigating dysfunctional systems

The UK has one of the lowest homeownership rates in Europe.

Half of England's land is owned by less than one percent of the population.

Young people are leaving — not because they don't try, but because the system fails them.
