The United Kingdom scores 0.40 on the GL Index — a D grade. As one of the world's oldest democracies, the UK retains a deep constitutional tradition and an independent judiciary. However, these institutional shells have been hollowed out — 70% of land remains in the hands of aristocratic landlords, the House of Lords retains hereditary seats, and both parties (Conservative and Labour) rotate in power but never touch the land structure. Value is filtered by the aristocracy, developers, and financial capital, never returning to the people. This is the "tenants move in and out, the landlord never changes" model of governance.
