From Procedure to Delivery:
The Global Procurement Revolution

从程序到交付：全球采购革命如何终结“信息不对称”的旧秩序
August 2026 · 2026年8月

Executive Order 14402, issued by the White House on April 30, 2026, has triggered a global shift in public procurement. The old logic—process compliance—is being replaced by a new imperative: outcome delivery.

This is not a minor adjustment. It is a fundamental governance paradigm shift: from procedure to delivery.

I. The End of an Era

In the summer of 2026, a quiet earthquake struck global public procurement. On April 30, the White House signed EO 14402. In July, China’s revised Government Procurement Law was released for public comment, and the “one‑network” reform for state‑owned enterprises took full effect. Germany, Australia, the United Kingdom, Canada—almost simultaneously, governments around the world were doing the same thing: restructuring the logic of procurement.

This is no coincidence. It is a global paradigm shift from “process compliance” to “outcome delivery.”

And for those professionals and intermediaries who have thrived on “information asymmetry,” this may be the worst of times.

II. The Old Order: Information Asymmetry and Procedural Hegemony

Under the traditional Western governance model, procurement was essentially about “procedural justice.” Governments proved they were “not corrupt” through complex bidding processes, voluminous compliance requirements, and thousand‑page contracts.

But this system had a fatal by‑product: it created massive information asymmetry.

  • Those who could read complex regulations won bids;
  • Those who could interpret vague clauses charged high fees;
  • Those who mastered bureaucratic processes survived.

Expertise became a barrier; procedural complexity became a moat. Thus, a vast “compliance consulting industry” was born—advisors profited by interpreting rules, writing proposals, and navigating audits, rather than by delivering real value.

In the U.S. alone, cost‑reimbursement consulting contracts reached $120 billion in fiscal year 2024. Governments spent fortunes, yet often failed to buy the outcomes they wanted.

III. EO 14402: The Blade That Tore Open the Procedural Iron Curtain

EO 14402 mandated that federal agencies prioritize fixed‑price contracts over cost‑reimbursement models. Its core message is stark: “We pay for results, not for costs.”

This fundamentally changes risk allocation and incentives:

Old Model (Cost‑Reimbursement)New Model (Fixed‑Price)
Government bears all cost overrunsContractor bears the risk
Profit tied to cost — slower and more expensive means more profitProfit tied to performance — faster and better means more profit
Incentivizes complexity and delayIncentivizes simplicity and delivery
Profits from information asymmetryProfits from delivery capability

EO 14402’s logic is: you don’t need to tell us how you do it—you just need to show us what you achieved. If you fail, that’s your risk, not the taxpayer’s.

“The era of cost‑plus complacency is over. Performance is the only currency.”

— White House OMB Memo, May 2026

IV. Global Resonance: A Collective Turn in Summer 2026

The U.S. executive order was not an isolated event. In July 2026, governments acted in concert:

The common thread: process must serve delivery; compliance must lead to performance.

V. China’s Distinctive Logic: Intellectual Property Is Not a Moat

Among these global reforms, China’s approach is particularly unique. For a long time, China has held a pragmatic stance toward “information asymmetry” and “intellectual property”:

Intellectual property is essentially an extension of private property under capitalism—it creates artificial scarcity to extract monopoly rents.

But within China’s governance framework, this “scarcity” often yields to:

  • Scale‑up application — enabling advanced technologies to serve the public good;
  • Industrial chain synergy — open ecosystems drive global market share through iteration speed;
  • National security and people’s livelihoods — mobilizing resources to overcome “bottleneck” technologies regardless of short‑term costs.

Thus, in China:

  • Patents are merely entry tickets, not moats;
  • “I know” is less valuable than “I can make it happen”;
  • Consultants who survive by interpreting rules are being displaced by digital platforms.

The July 2026 “one‑network” reform for SOEs is the institutional embodiment of this logic: all information on‑chain, all processes open, all results traceable. Information asymmetry is being eliminated; professional barriers are crumbling.

VI. A GFI Framework Conclusion: Governance Legitimacy = Delivery Capability

Through the lens of the Governance Legitimacy Framework (GFI), the conclusion is clear:

Old ParadigmNew Paradigm
Procedural justiceOutcome justice
Compliance‑orientedDelivery‑oriented
Information asymmetryInformation transparency
IP as a moatIP as an entry ticket
Governance = process managementGovernance = value creation

Governance legitimacy is no longer derived from “whether procedures are followed,” but from “whether the system can consistently deliver outcomes.”

This is the underlying philosophy of EO 14402, China’s July reforms, and the global procurement revolution.

VII. What This Means for You

If you are a policymaker:
Embed “outcome verification” into the entire procurement lifecycle; eliminate information black boxes; let data speak.

If you are a vendor or professional:
Abandon the old mindset of “profiting from interpreting rules.” Translate your knowledge into verifiable delivery capability. Remember: process is a means; delivery is the end.

If you are a citizen:
You have the right to know what your tax dollars have achieved—not just a signed contract.

Conclusion: Dawn of the Delivery Era

The global procurement reforms of summer 2026 send a clear signal:

“The old order is over. The future belongs to those who can deliver outcomes—not just follow procedures.”

In China, this means:

  • Intellectual property is no longer a permanent moat;
  • Information asymmetry is no longer a profit tool;
  • The only sustainable competitive advantage is your ability to translate knowledge into value.

The world is entering the “Delivery Era”—an era that says no to empty talk and arbitrage, and yes to action and results.

第14402号行政令，由白宫于2026年4月30日签署，引发了全球公共采购的方向性转变。旧的逻辑——程序合规——正在被新的要求所取代：成果交付。

这不是一次微调，而是一场根本性的治理范式转移：从程序到交付。

一、序章：一个时代的终结

2026年的夏天，全球公共采购领域发生了静悄悄的地震。4月30日，白宫签署了第14402号行政令。7月，中国《政府采购法》修订草案公开征求意见，国企“一网交易”改革全面落地。德国、澳大利亚、英国、加拿大……几乎在同一时间，各国政府都在做同一件事：重构采购逻辑。

这不是巧合。这是一场从 “程序合规” 到 “成果交付” 的全球范式转移。

而对于那些依靠 “信息不对称” 生存的专业人士和中间商来说——这可能是最坏的时代。

二、旧秩序的基石：信息不对称与程序霸权

在过去几十年的西方治理模式下，采购的本质是 “程序正义”。政府通过复杂的招标流程、繁冗的合规要求、厚达千页的合同条款来证明自己“没有腐败”。

但这套体系有一个致命的副产品：它创造了巨大的信息不对称。

  • 谁能读懂复杂的法规，谁就能中标；
  • 谁能解释模糊的条款，谁就能收费；
  • 谁能熟悉官僚的流程，谁就能生存。

专业知识变成了壁垒，流程复杂性变成了护城河。 于是，诞生了一个庞大的“合规咨询产业”——顾问们靠解读规则、撰写标书、应对审计来获利，而不是靠真正交付价值。

在美国，仅2024财年，成本报销型咨询合同就高达 1200亿美元。政府花了大钱，却常常买不到真正想要的结果。

三、EO 14402：撕开程序铁幕的利刃

第14402号行政令 要求联邦机构优先采用 固定价格合同，取代成本报销模式。其核心信息直白：“我们为成果付费，不为成本买单。”

这从根本上改变了风险分配和激励结构：

旧模式（成本报销）新模式（固定价格）
政府承担所有成本超支风险承包商承担风险
利润与成本挂钩，越慢越贵越赚钱利润与绩效挂钩，越快越好越赚钱
激励复杂化、延长化激励简化、交付化
靠信息不对称获利靠交付能力获利

EO 14402 的逻辑是：你不需要告诉我你怎么做，你只需要告诉我你做成了什么。 如果你做不成，那是你的风险，不是纳税人的风险。

“成本加成式的自满时代已经结束。绩效才是唯一的硬通货。”

— 白宫管理与预算办公室备忘录，2026年5月

四、全球共振：2026年夏天的集体转向

美国的行政令并不是孤例。2026年7月，各国政府同步行动：

国家改革措施核心逻辑
🇺🇸 美国EO 14402 固定价格强制令成果验证优先
🇨🇳 中国《政府采购法》修订 + 国企“一网交易”全流程透明 + 履约验收强化
🇦🇺 澳大利亚数字卖方绩效政策严重不履约的结构化问责
🇩🇪 德国公共采购加速法简化流程，加速交付
🇬🇧 英国采购修订条例 + “构建黄金标准”成果导向验证
🇪🇺 欧盟公共采购绩效衡量框架从“低价”转向“性价比”

这些改革的共同特征是：程序必须服务于交付，合规必须导向成效。

五、中国的特殊逻辑：知识产权不构成护城河

在这一轮全球改革中，中国的逻辑尤为独特。长期以来，中国对“信息不对称”和“知识产权”持有一种务实主义的立场：

知识产权的本质是资本主义私有制的延伸——制造人为稀缺，获取垄断利润。

但在中国的治理框架下，这种“稀缺”常常让位于：

  • 规模化应用 —— 让先进技术快速普及，服务全民福祉；
  • 产业链协同 —— 开放生态，靠迭代速度获取全球份额；
  • 国家安全与民生保障 —— 集中力量突破“卡脖子”技术，不计短期成本。

因此，在中国：

  • 专利只是入场券，不是护城河；
  • “我知道”不值钱，“我能做成”才值钱；
  • 靠解读规则生存的顾问，正在被数字化平台取代。

2026年7月国企“一网交易”改革，正是这一逻辑的制度化体现：所有信息上链、所有流程公开、所有结果可追溯。 信息不对称被消灭，专业壁垒被瓦解。

治理的正当性，不再来自于“程序是否合规”，而来自于“系统是否能够持续交付成果”。

这正是 EO 14402、中国7月改革以及全球采购革命的底层哲学。

七、给读者的启示

如果你是政策制定者：
将“成果验证”嵌入采购全流程；消灭信息黑箱，让数据说话。

如果你是供应商或专业人士：
放弃“靠解读规则获利”的旧思维；将你的知识转化为可验证的交付能力。记住：程序是手段，交付才是目的。

如果你是公民：
你有权知道：你的税款换来了什么成果，而不仅仅是一份合同。

结语：交付时代的黎明

2026年夏天的全球采购改革，释放了一个明确的信号：

“旧秩序结束了。未来属于那些能够交付成果的人，而非仅仅遵循程序的人。”

在中国，这意味着：

  • 知识产权不再是永久护城河；
  • 信息不对称不再是盈利工具；
  • 唯一可持续的竞争优势，是你将知识转化为价值的能力。

全球正在进入 “交付时代” ——一个对空谈和套利说不，对实干和成果说是的时代。
