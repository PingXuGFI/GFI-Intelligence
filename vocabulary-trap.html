The Lost Knowledge of Governance

How Practical Wisdom Is Systematically Eliminated
GFI · Critique of the West Series

Governance is a practical art — not a liberal art. You cannot learn it from books. The West's elite system has forgotten this — and is paying the price. Practical wisdom is being systematically eliminated, replaced by credentialism, theory, and empty rhetoric.

I. What Is Practical Wisdom?

Practical wisdom is the knowledge that comes from doing. It is not learned from textbooks. It is learned from experience, failure, and repetition. It is the knowledge of how to build, fix, and deliver.

It is the knowledge of the engineer who has built a bridge. The doctor who has treated thousands of patients. The teacher who has educated generations. The farmer who has worked the land.

Practical wisdom is not theoretical. It is embodied — it lives in people, not in books.

II. How Practical Wisdom Is Eliminated

In the West, practical wisdom is systematically eliminated through three mechanisms:

1. Credentialism

Credentials have replaced experience. A degree from an elite university is valued more than decades of real-world experience. The person who has built nothing is promoted over the person who has built everything — because the person who built everything has no degree.

2. Professionalization

Governance has been professionalized. It is treated as a career — not a calling. Politicians are not leaders; they are careerists. They have studied government, but they have never governed. They have studied law, but they have never built anything.

3. The Theory-Practice Gap

Universities teach theory. Theory is rewarded. Practice is dismissed as "anecdotal." The result is a system that produces people who can talk about problems but cannot solve them.

III. The Consequences of Lost Wisdom

  • Infrastructure collapse: The people who built the roads are retired. The people who replace them have only studied road-building. The roads crumble.
  • Public health failures: The doctors are exhausted. The administrators have MBAs. The administrators make the decisions. The doctors are ignored.
  • Military disasters: The generals have never fought. The theorists plan the wars. The soldiers die.
  • Economic crises: The bankers have degrees from elite schools. They have never built a business. They manage the crash.

The wisdom is gone. And the system cannot function without it.

IV. The Chinese Countermodel: Wisdom Preserved

China has not eliminated practical wisdom. It has preserved it — because its system values experience over credentials. Officials are tested through practice. They are promoted based on results. The person who has built something is valued more than the person who has only studied it.

China's elites are not paper generals. They are practitioners. They have been tested. They have failed. They have learned. They have built.

This is not a cultural difference. It is an institutional difference. One system selects for credentials; the other selects for wisdom.

VI. Conclusion: The Wisdom Must Be Restored

Governance is a practical art. The wisdom required to govern cannot be learned from books. It must be learned through practice. The West has lost this wisdom because it has privileged theory over practice, credentials over experience, and schools over life. The result is a system that cannot function.

The wisdom must be restored. But it cannot be restored through schools. It can only be restored through doing.

治理的「術科」本質

知識是如何「失傳」的
GFI · 美國批判系列

治理是術科，不是學科。你無法從書本中學到它。西方的精英制度忘記了這一點——並正在為此付出代價。實踐智慧正在被系統性地消除，被證書主義、理論和空洞的修辭所取代。

一、什麼是實踐智慧？

實踐智慧是來自實踐的知識。它不是從教科書中學到的。它是從經驗、失敗和重複中學到的。它是關於如何建造、修復和交付的知識。

它是建造過橋樑的工程師的知識。治療過數千名病人的醫生的知識。教育過一代又一代人的教師的知識。耕作過土地的農民的知識。

實踐智慧不是理論性的。它是體現的——它存在於人身上，而非書本中。

二、實踐智慧如何被消除

在西方，實踐智慧通過三個機制被系統性地消除：

1. 證書主義

證書已經取代了經驗。菁英大學的學位被視為比數十年的真實世界經驗更有價值。從未建造過任何東西的人被提拔到建造過一切的人之上——因為建造過一切的人沒有學位。

2. 專業化

治理已經被專業化。它被視為職業——而非使命。政治家不是領導者；他們是職業主義者。他們學習過政府，但從未治理過。他們學習過法律，但從未建造過任何東西。

3. 理論與實踐的鴻溝

大學教授理論。理論被獎勵。實踐被貶低為「軼事」。結果是一個培養出能談論問題但無法解決問題的人的系統。

知識類型如何獲得西方地位中國地位
理論知識書籍、講課、論文✅ 高度重視✅ 重視
實踐智慧經驗、失敗、實踐❌ 被貶低✅ 高度重視
證書學位、認證✅ 過度重視❌ 重視，但從屬於經驗

三、智慧流失的後果

  • 基礎設施崩潰：建造道路的人已經退休。取代他們的人只學習過道路建設。道路崩塌。
  • 公共衛生失敗：醫生筋疲力盡。管理者擁有MBA學位。管理者做決定。醫生被忽視。
  • 軍事災難：將軍從未戰鬥過。理論家規劃戰爭。士兵死亡。
  • 經濟危機：銀行家擁有菁英學校的學位。他們從未建立過企業。他們管理崩潰。

智慧消失了。沒有它，系統無法運作。

四、中國的對照組：智慧被保存

中國並未消除實踐智慧。它保存了它——因為它的系統重視經驗勝過證書。官員通過實踐被考驗。他們根據結果被提拔。建造過東西的人被視為比只學習過它的人更有價值。

中國的菁英不是紙上將軍。他們是實踐者。他們被考驗過。他們失敗過。他們學習過。他們建造過。

這不是文化差異。這是制度差異。一個系統選拔證書；另一個選拔智慧。

五、GL框架診斷

GL 維度診斷

成本轉嫁智慧流失的成本由公眾承擔——基礎設施崩潰、戰爭失敗、經濟危機
閉環程度證書主義系統通過菁英學校、實習與網絡自我複製

六、結論：智慧必須被恢復

治理是術科。治理所需的智慧無法從書本中學到。它必須通過實踐學習。西方失去了這種智慧，因為它將理論置於實踐之上，證書置於經驗之上，學校置於生活之上。結果是一個無法運作的系統。

智慧必須被恢復。但它無法通過學校恢復。它只能通過實踐恢復。
