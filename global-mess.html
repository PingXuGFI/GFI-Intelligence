The Structural Necessity of GL
Why independent outcome verification is no longer optional

1. Governments can no longer prove value for money

This is the single largest political risk. The UK, Canada, the US, and Australia have all entered procurement accountability, public spending scrutiny, AI governance, and modernization review phases.

2. The AI era amplifies error

In the past, low efficiency meant slowness. Now, automated error means mass misclassification, mass wrongful denial, mass incorrect debt. Australia's Robodebt is the warning.

3. No existing governance role fills this gap

We have audit, procurement, PMO, cybersecurity, compliance, QA, IV&V. But no one verifies whether citizens can actually complete things. This is the vacancy.

4. EO 14402 is the signal

Executive Order 14402 is about efficiency, accountability, measurable performance, contractor scrutiny. Stop telling me what you did. Tell me whether outcomes actually improved.
