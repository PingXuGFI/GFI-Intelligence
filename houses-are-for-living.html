The Truth of the Sub‑Landlord System

How Western Democracy Functions as a 'Sub‑Landlord' System
GFI · Land & Housing Series

Western democracy does not challenge the landlord class. It reinforces it — by creating a layer of "sub‑landlords" who extract rent on behalf of the system. The sub‑landlord is not the ultimate owner — but they perform the same function: extracting value from the many and transferring it upward.

I. What Is the Sub‑Landlord System?

The sub‑landlord system is a hierarchical extraction chain. At the top is the ultimate landowner — the state, the corporation, or the aristocracy. Below them are sub‑landlords — intermediaries who manage the extraction of rent. At the bottom are the tenants — the people who actually pay.

In Western democracies, the sub‑landlord system operates through:

  • Property management companies — extracting rent on behalf of absentee landlords
  • Real estate investment trusts (REITs) — extracting value through financialized rental markets
  • Government agencies — extracting value through property taxes and fees
  • Financial institutions — extracting value through mortgage interest and fees

III. The Political Function: Disguising Ownership

The sub‑landlord system serves a political purpose: it disguises who actually owns the land. The ultimate owners remain hidden behind layers of intermediaries. The tenant pays rent to a property manager, who pays a REIT, which pays dividends to anonymous shareholders.

The system is opaque by design. If people knew who owned the land, they would demand change. The sub‑landlord system keeps the truth hidden.

VI. Conclusion: Name the Extraction Chain

The sub‑landlord system is not an accident. It is a design — designed to extract value from the many while hiding who actually benefits. The first step to ending it is to name it — to trace the extraction chain from tenant to ultimate owner. Once the chain is visible, it can be broken.

The sub‑landlord system is not governance. It is extraction with a smile.

二房東制度的真相

西方民主如何作為「二房東」系統運作
GFI · 土地與住房系列

西方民主並不挑戰地主階級。它強化它——通過創造一層「二房東」，代表系統提取租金。二房東不是最終所有者——但它們執行相同的功能：從多數人提取價值並向上轉移。

一、什麼是二房東制度？

二房東制度是一個層級化的提取鏈。頂端是終極地主——國家、公司或貴族。下面是二房東——管理租金提取的中介。最底層是租戶——實際支付的人。

在西方民主國家中，二房東制度通過以下方式運作：

  • 物業管理公司——代表缺席地主提取租金
  • 房地產投資信託（REITs）——通過金融化的租賃市場提取價值
  • 政府機構——通過房產稅和費用提取價值
  • 金融機構——通過抵押貸款利息和費用提取價值

二、二房東功能：沒有所有權的提取

角色做什麼服務誰剝削誰
地主擁有土地，提取租金自己 / 股東租戶
二房東（物業管理）代表所有者管理提取地主租戶
二房東（金融機構）提取利息和費用投資者購房者、租戶
二房東（政府）提取稅收和費用國家預算業主、租戶

三、政治功能：隱藏所有權

二房東制度服務於一個政治目的：它掩飾誰真正擁有土地。最終所有者隱藏在多層中介後面。租戶向物業經理支付租金，物業經理付給REIT，REIT向匿名股東分紅。

系統是故意不透明的。如果人們知道誰擁有土地，他們會要求改變。二房東制度將真相隱藏起來。

四、二房東制度的實踐

英國物業管理公司租金提取、費用租戶將40%+收入用於住房

德國企業地主租金提取租戶將30%+收入用於住房

六、結論：命名提取鏈

二房東制度不是偶然的。它是一個設計——設計來從多數人提取價值，同時隱藏誰真正受益。終結它的第一步是命名它——追溯從租戶到最終所有者的提取鏈。一旦鏈條可視化，它就能被打破。

二房東制度不是治理。它是帶著微笑的提取。
