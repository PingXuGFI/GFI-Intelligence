Why a physical instrument?

Why not an app? Why not a website? Why not SaaS?

Because the federal market doesn't buy SaaS from startups.

Any software sold to the U.S. federal government must pass FedRAMP — a certification process that takes 12–18 months, costs $250,000–$1,000,000, and requires compliance with hundreds of NIST security controls.

GL Verifier bypasses all of that. It is a physical, offline instrument. It does not collect, store, or transmit data. It is a calculator with a screen. No FedRAMP. No security audit. No waiting.
