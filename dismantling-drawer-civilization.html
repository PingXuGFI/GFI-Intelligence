Dismantling Drawer Civilization

From Classification Traps to Human Networks
Ping Xu · 2025 · ~15 min read 📖 NEW BOOK
📥 Download Full Book PDF

We live in a world structured to manage complexity, yet in doing so, it has sacrificed humanity itself. The systems around us—welfare, health, education, bureaucracy, international institutions—appear neutral and rational, but they operate according to a single logic: the drawer.

Drawer civilization is not a conspiracy. No shadowy elite sits in a control room deciding your fate. It is a self-protecting mechanism of an outdated structure. It emerged to increase efficiency, to make complex societies manageable — but in the 21st century, it has become lethal.

「The more refined the drawers, the easier people fall through them.」

📦 The Birth of Drawers — How Classification Became a Tool of Rule

Classification was originally a tool, but gradually became the purpose. When the world was broken into components, people were also broken into components: Which class do you belong to? Which department? Which qualification? Which drawer?

The three operating principles of drawer civilization were thus formed:

  • Classification first: Before listening to the problem, first ask which category you belong to.
  • Formatting processing: Place in the corresponding drawer, system declares "processed."
  • Responsibility shifting: After the drawer is closed, no one is responsible for what happens inside.

🌍 Global Samples of Drawer Civilization

European Model — Welfare States: Precise eligibility, automated payments. Non-standard families or migrants often excluded.

U.S. Model — Fragmented Systems: Federal-state-county layering, inconsistent rules. Families lose support when moving or switching programs.

Asian Model — High-Density Cities: Efficiency, automation, digital ID. Lonely deaths, overwork collapse; payments handled, connections ignored.

UN Model — International Institutions: Member classification, project eligibility, donation tiers. Slow response to crises; survival delayed.

🎭 Illusion One — "Classification Complete Means Problem Solved"

Drawer civilization thrives on processing: if a person is put into the correct drawer, the system declares "handled." But being "handled" rarely equals being "helped."

⚖️ Illusion Two — "Systems Are Neutral"

No system is neutral. Every system protects, ignores, or avoids responsibility based on structural incentives, historical hierarchies, and resource allocations.

🧠 Illusion Three — "Individual Failure Explains Everything"

This illusion shifts responsibility from systems to individuals. It allows society to avoid accountability while maintaining the appearance of order.

💀 Chronic Murder — Economic Despair and Psychological Collapse

Drawer civilization does not kill with a single blow. It kills slowly, invisibly, systematically.

🧱 Physical and Social Collapse

Constant stress and systemic neglect manifest physically. Financial instability, lack of healthcare access, and bureaucratic delays increase hypertension, heart disease, and stroke risk.

🔧 From Drawers to Networks — Design Principles

PRINCIPLE 1

Connection Over Classification

Move from "Which drawer do you belong to?" to "How are you connected to resources and people?"

PRINCIPLE 2

Responsibility Over Avoidance

Without responsibility, systemic failure hides behind rules. Assign accountable nodes instead of anonymous drawers.

PRINCIPLE 3

Immediate Support Over Eligibility Delays

Survival is primary; bureaucracy secondary. Provide instant access to shelter, food, and medical attention.

PRINCIPLE 4

Contribution Over Compliance

Recognition, reward, and survival must be tied to real actions, not certificates, status, or arbitrary classifications.

PRINCIPLE 5

Financial Sovereignty

Establish community-controlled funds. Encourage universal basic resources. Reduce reliance on hierarchical approvals for life-critical needs.

🗺️ Implementation Roadmap — Three Phases to Smash Drawers

Phase 1 — Emergency Survival Floor: Immediate access to shelter, food, water, and basic medical aid without eligibility checks.

Phase 2 — Contribution System: Replace rigid eligibility with verifiable contribution metrics.

Phase 3 — Global Commons Governance: Replace hierarchical, donor-dependent structures with collaborative global governance.

🔄 Psychological and Cultural Transformation

Smashing drawers is not only structural — it is also psychological. Drawer civilization trains fear: fear of stepping out, fear of responsibility, fear of failure.

🔥 Conclusion: Burn the Drawers, Fly Up

Drawer civilization is ending. Not with a bang, not with a moral sermon, but quietly, through the cumulative failure to safeguard life, opportunity, and dignity.

To survive — and to thrive — we must burn the drawers. The choice is simple: continue to fall into drawers, or rise above them.

Burn the drawers. Fly up.

拆掉抽屜文明

從分類陷阱到人類網絡
徐萍 · 2025 · 閱讀時間約 15 分鐘 📖 新書
📥 下載全書 PDF

我們生活在一個為管理複雜性而設計的世界，但正因如此，它犧牲了人性。圍繞我們的系統——社福、醫療、教育、官僚體系、國際機構——看似中立且理性，實則遵循同一種邏輯：抽屜。

抽屜文明不是陰謀。沒有神秘精英坐在控制室決定你的命運。它是一個過時結構的自我保護機制。它的誕生是為了提高效率，讓複雜社會可管理——但到了21世紀，它變得致命。

「抽屜越精緻，人越容易掉進縫隙。」

📦 抽屜的誕生——分類如何成為統治工具

分類原本是工具，但逐漸變成了目的。當世界被拆解成零件時，人也被拆解成零件。

  • 分類優先：問題還沒聽完，先問你屬於哪一類。
  • 格式化處理：放進對應抽屜，系統宣告「已處理」。
  • 責任推卸：抽屜關上後，裡面發生什麼，沒人負責。

🌍 抽屜文明的全球樣本

歐洲模式——福利國家：精確資格、自動給付。非標準家庭或移民易被排除。

美國模式——碎片系統：聯邦-州-郡分層，規則不一。

亞洲模式——高密度都市：高效率、自動化、數位身分。孤獨死、過勞死。

聯合國模式——國際機構：成員分類、專案資格、捐款等級。危機反應慢。

🎭 幻覺一——「分類完成等於問題解決」

⚖️ 幻覺二——「制度是中立的」

🧠 幻覺三——「個人失敗解釋一切」

💀 慢性謀殺——經濟絕境與心理崩壞

🧱 身體與社會的崩壞

原則一

連結優先，超越分類

從「你屬於哪個抽屜？」轉變為「你如何與資源和他人連結？」

原則二

承擔責任，拒絕推卸

沒有責任，制度失靈隱藏在規則背後。

原則三

即時支援，超越資格審核

生存優先；官僚次之。

原則四

貢獻驗證，取代資格壟斷

認可與生存應與實際行動掛鉤。

原則五

財務主權

建立社群掌控的資金。推動普惠基本資源。

🗺️ 實施路線圖——三階段拆解抽屜

第一階段——緊急生存底線

第二階段——貢獻系統

第三階段——全球公域治理

🔥 結語：燒掉抽屜，飛起來

抽屜文明正在結束。不是轟然一聲，也不是道德說教，而是因為它無法保障生命、機會與尊嚴的累積失敗，悄然瓦解。

要生存——甚至蓬勃生長——我們必須燒掉抽屜。選擇很簡單：繼續掉進抽屜，或者超越抽屜。

燒掉抽屜。飛起來。
