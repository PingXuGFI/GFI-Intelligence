Task Force Mission

The task force identifies administrative friction, hidden implementation failure, procurement acceptance gaps, and AI-driven error amplification before they become permanent infrastructure.

Diagnose Friction

Map citizen journeys, repeated submissions, queue structures, escalation points, and operational burden that conventional dashboards often miss.

Detect False Digitization

Identify systems that digitize the appearance of service delivery without simplifying workflow logic or reducing citizen burden.

Verify Outcomes

Compare before-and-after evidence using observable metrics such as processing time, completion rates, abandonment, appeals, and service activation speed.

Operational Independence

The task force must remain structurally independent from implementation vendors, system integrators, software providers, and internal self-evaluation teams.

Not Vendor QA
The task force does not validate the vendor’s own claims or substitute for internal quality assurance.
Not Implementation Consulting
The task force does not build, resell, or implement the systems it evaluates.
Not Dashboard Reporting
The task force evaluates whether reported metrics correspond to real-world service improvement.
Not Political Messaging
The task force does not modify findings for political convenience or communications strategy.

90-Day Rapid Diagnostic Model

A practical diagnostic structure for governments that need fast, independent visibility into whether a program, system, or AI deployment is producing measurable public value.

Phase 1 · Weeks 1–2

Intake

Collect workflow materials, public complaints, procurement promises, service metrics, and system entry points.

Phase 2 · Weeks 3–6

Field Observation

Test citizen journeys, map friction, observe live process behavior, and identify false digitization patterns.

Phase 3 · Weeks 7–10

Outcome Measurement

Compare before-and-after conditions, quantify burden, evaluate completion flow, and validate outcome claims.

Phase 4 · Weeks 11–12

Independent Findings

Produce final verification report, procurement risk findings, and reform recommendations.

Diagnostic Domains

Every task force engagement evaluates the public system across seven domains that connect citizen experience to procurement accountability.

Administrative Friction
Time, repetition, delay, complexity, unnecessary steps, and avoidable burden.
Citizen Burden
Human effort required to access, complete, correct, appeal, or sustain a public service interaction.
Workflow Integrity
Whether the process logic is coherent, traceable, and aligned with the stated service purpose.
False Digitization
Digital surfaces that preserve manual bottlenecks, paper logic, duplicated entry, or hidden staff rework.
AI Placement
Whether AI is placed in a way that reduces burden or amplifies procedural error and exclusion.
Procurement Accountability
Whether acceptance criteria verify real outcomes or only vendor deliverables.
Outcome Visibility
Whether measurable public impact can be independently observed after deployment.

Standard Deliverables

The task force produces documents that can be used by agency leadership, procurement officers, oversight bodies, public administrators, and implementation partners.

Executive Brief

A leadership-level summary of findings, operational risks, public consequences, and recommended actions.

GL Diagnostic Report

A formal analytical report covering workflow analysis, friction mapping, outcome verification, and governance risk.

Public Accountability Summary

A simplified public-facing explanation of what changed, what failed, and whether outcomes improved.
