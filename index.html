The Problem

Most systems are implemented.
Very few are verified.

Digital transformation projects are accepted based on system delivery — but rarely verified based on real-world outcomes. Procurement closes when the system goes live. Whether it works is never formally required.

"The system was delivered.
The outcome was never required."
