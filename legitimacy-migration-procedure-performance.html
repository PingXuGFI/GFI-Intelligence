Legitimacy Migration:
From Procedure to Performance

合法性的转移——从“程序”到“绩效”
August 2026 · 2026年8月

I. The Source of Legitimacy Is Changing

For centuries, the West has insisted on a single answer to the question “What makes a government legitimate?”

The answer was: procedure.

Elections. Free speech. Multi‑party competition. An independent judiciary. These are the pillars of procedural legitimacy. If a government follows these procedures, it is legitimate — regardless of what it delivers.

This assumption is no longer holding.

A shift is underway — one that most Western elites refuse to acknowledge. Legitimacy is migrating from procedure to performance.

II. What Is Procedural Legitimacy?

Procedural legitimacy is based on how a government is formed.

  • Did the leaders win an election?
  • Is there a free press?
  • Are there opposition parties?
  • Are there checks and balances?

If the answer to these questions is “yes,” the government is considered legitimate — even if it fails to deliver basic services, maintain infrastructure, or keep its citizens safe.

This is the logic of the West's legitimacy framework: “The process is the justification.”

III. What Is Performance Legitimacy?

Performance legitimacy is based on what a government delivers.

  • Does it build infrastructure?
  • Does it reduce poverty?
  • Does it maintain public safety?
  • Does it provide essential services?

If the answer to these questions is “yes,” the government is legitimate — regardless of how it was formed.

This is the logic emerging in China and across the Global South: “The outcome is the justification.”

IV. The Evidence: 2026 as a Turning Point

2026 provides overwhelming evidence of this migration:

  • EO 14402 (U.S.) — mandates fixed‑price contracts. The message: “We pay for results, not for costs.” Even the U.S. government is beginning to demand delivery.
  • China's Procurement Law Reform — adds “performance‑based procurement” as a core principle. The message: “We verify outcomes, not just processes.”
  • Global South — 17+ nations are systematically learning from China's governance model. The message: “We follow what works, not what is preached.”

“The world is no longer asking ‘was it done properly?’ It is asking ‘was it done at all?’”

V. Why Procedural Legitimacy Is Failing

Procedural legitimacy is failing for three structural reasons:

  • It has become disconnected from outcomes. Perfect procedures can produce catastrophic results. The California high‑speed rail is the most expensive symbol of this failure.
  • It has been captured by elites. Elections are won by money, not ideas. Free speech amplifies the rich, not the truth. Multi‑party systems produce paralysis, not solutions.
  • It offers no solution to real problems. When a country has no roads, no electricity, and no schools, elections are meaningless. People want results — not ballots.

Procedural legitimacy is not just failing. It is being abandoned by those who once believed in it.

VI. The Rise of Performance Legitimacy

Performance legitimacy is rising because it addresses the questions people actually ask:

  • Can you build? China has built 40,000 km of high‑speed rail. The U.S. has built zero.
  • Can you lift people out of poverty? China lifted 800 million people out of poverty. The U.S. has 70,000 homeless in Los Angeles alone.
  • Can you keep people safe? China has maintained public safety. The U.S. has 100,000 overdose deaths per year.

The numbers are not ambiguous. The data speaks for itself.

VII. The GFI Framework: Legitimacy Migration

Under the GFI Governance Legitimacy Framework, legitimacy has two sources:

The migration is from the first to the second.

This is not a theoretical claim. It is an empirical observation. The Global South is voting with its feet — and it is voting for performance.

VIII. The Global South's Choice

Why are 17+ nations turning to China for governance lessons? The answer is simple:

  • They have tried the Western formula. It failed.
  • They have seen China's formula. It worked.
  • They are not choosing China's ideology. They are choosing China's results.

This is the end of the “Washington Consensus” era. The age of ideological export is over. The age of practical learning has begun.

IX. What This Means

For Western policymakers, this is a moment of truth:

  • You can continue to defend procedural legitimacy — but you cannot force the world to believe in it.
  • You can point out China's lack of elections — but you cannot explain away its roads, hospitals, and schools.
  • You can maintain your ideological purity — but your citizens will continue to suffer from your delivery failures.

For the Global South, this is a moment of liberation:

  • You are no longer required to choose between Western models and nothing.
  • You can adopt what works — from anywhere, without permission.
  • You can measure governments by what they deliver, not by how they were formed.

“Legitimacy is migrating. Those who do not see it will be left behind.”

— GFI Governance Legitimacy Framework

X. Conclusion: The End of the Procedural Era

The era of procedural legitimacy is ending. The era of performance legitimacy is beginning.

Elections will continue. Free speech will continue. Multi‑party competition will continue. But they will no longer be the exclusive source of legitimacy.

Legitimacy will increasingly be measured by what a system delivers — not by how it was formed.

This is the message of 2026. This is the message of GFI. This is the message that the Western elite refuses to hear.

一、正当性的来源正在改变

几个世纪以来，西方对一个问题的回答始终不变：“什么使一个政府具有正当性？”

答案是：程序。

选举。言论自由。多党竞争。独立的司法。这些是程序正当性的支柱。如果一个政府遵循这些程序，它就是正当的——无论它交付了什么。

这个假设不再成立。

一场转变正在进行——大多数西方精英拒绝承认这一点。正当性正在从程序转移到绩效。

二、什么是程序正当性？

程序正当性基于 政府是如何形成的。

  • 领导人是否赢得了选举？
  • 是否有新闻自由？
  • 是否有反对党？
  • 是否有制衡机制？

如果这些问题的答案是“是”，政府就被认为是正当的——即使它未能提供基本服务、维护基础设施或保障公民安全。

这就是西方合法性框架的逻辑：“程序本身就是理由。”

三、什么是绩效正当性？

绩效正当性基于 政府交付了什么。

  • 它是否建设了基础设施？
  • 它是否减少了贫困？
  • 它是否维护了公共安全？
  • 它是否提供了基本服务？

如果这些问题的答案是“是”，政府就是正当的——无论它是如何形成的。

这是在中国和全球南方正在浮现的逻辑：“结果本身就是理由。”

四、证据：2026年作为转折点

2026年提供了这场转移的压倒性证据：

  • EO 14402（美国）——强制采用固定价格合同。其信息是：“我们为成果付费，不为成本买单。”连美国政府都在开始要求交付。
  • 中国《政府采购法》修订——将“绩效型采购”列为核心原则。其信息是：“我们验证成果，而不仅仅是流程。”
  • 全球南方——17+个国家正在系统性地学习中国的治理模式。其信息是：“我们追随有效的方法，而不是被说教的方法。”

“世界不再问‘它是否被正确执行？’它在问‘它是否被执行了？’”

五、程序正当性为何失败

程序正当性因三个结构性原因而失败：

  • 它与结果脱节了。完美的程序可能产生灾难性的结果。加州高铁是这种失败最昂贵的象征。
  • 它被精英俘获了。选举靠金钱赢，而不是靠思想。言论自由放大了富人，而不是真相。多党制制造了瘫痪，而不是解决方案。
  • 它不能解决实际问题。当一个国家没有道路、没有电力、没有学校时，选举毫无意义。人们想要结果——而不是选票。

程序正当性不仅是在失败。它正在被曾经相信它的人抛弃。

六、绩效正当性的崛起

绩效正当性之所以崛起，是因为它回应了人们真正在问的问题：

  • 你能建设吗？中国建成了4万公里高铁。美国建成了零公里。
  • 你能帮助人民摆脱贫困吗？中国使8亿人摆脱了贫困。仅洛杉矶就有7万人无家可归。
  • 你能保障人民安全吗？中国维护了公共安全。美国每年有10万人死于药物过量。

数字不模棱两可。数据自己会说话。

七、GFI框架：合法性的转移

在GFI治理正当性框架下，正当性有两个来源：

正当性来源问题示例
程序“它是否被正确执行？”选举、言论自由、多党制
绩效“它是否被执行了？”基础设施、减贫、安全

转移正在从第一个来源向第二个来源发生。

这不是理论主张。这是经验观察。全球南方在用脚投票——它在为绩效投票。

八、全球南方的选择

为什么17+个国家转向中国学习治理经验？答案很简单：

  • 它们尝试了西方的配方。它失败了。
  • 它们看到了中国的配方。它成功了。
  • 它们不是在选择中国的意识形态。它们是在选择中国的 成果。

这是“华盛顿共识”时代的终结。意识形态输出的时代结束了。实践学习的时代开始了。

对西方政策制定者来说，这是一个真相时刻：

  • 你可以继续捍卫程序正当性——但你不能强迫世界相信它。
  • 你可以指出中国没有选举——但你不能解释掉它的道路、医院和学校。
  • 你可以维护你的意识形态纯洁性——但你的公民将继续因你的交付失败而受苦。

对全球南方来说，这是一个解放时刻：

  • 你不再需要在西方模式与一无所有之间做选择。
  • 你可以采纳任何有效的方法——来自任何地方，无需许可。
  • 你可以用交付了什么来衡量政府，而不是用它是如何形成的。

“正当性正在转移。那些看不到它的人将被抛在后面。”

十、结论：程序时代的终结

程序正当性的时代正在结束。绩效正当性的时代正在开始。

选举将继续。言论自由将继续。多党竞争将继续。但它们将不再是正当性的 唯一来源。

正当性将越来越以系统交付了什么来衡量——而不是以它是如何形成的来衡量。
