Land Policy is the Foundation of Governance

The Root of All Governance Legitimacy
GFI · Land & Housing Series

All governance legitimacy ultimately rests on one question: who owns the land? The answer determines who has power, who pays the cost, and who benefits from the system.

I. Why Land?

Land is not just another asset. It is the foundation of all wealth. Everything is built on land: housing, industry, agriculture, infrastructure. Without land, there is no economy. Without control of land, there is no control of power.

Throughout history, the ruling class has been the land-owning class. The feudal lords owned the land. The colonial empires owned the land. The modern oligarchs own the land.

Whoever controls the land controls the society.

II. How Land Determines Governance

III. The GL Framework Perspective

GL DimensionHow Land Policy Shapes Governance
Cost ShiftingPrivate land ownership shifts the cost of housing, infrastructure, and development onto the public
Transfer PathwaysLand value flows upward to landowners, not back to the community
Information AsymmetryLand ownership is often hidden; the public does not know who holds power
Closure IntensityLandowners form a closed class that uses its power to protect land ownership
Waiting CostSpeculation leaves land vacant while people wait for housing; the cost of waiting is borne by the homeless and the young

IV. China vs. The West: Two Models

  • China: Land is a public asset. The state allocates land for development. Speculation is controlled. Housing is for living.
  • The West: Land is a private commodity. Land is traded, speculated on, and hoarded. Housing is a profit center.

The difference is not economic. It is constitutional — it is written into the foundation of the system.

V. Conclusion: Land Policy is the Core

Every governance question eventually leads back to land. Who owns it? Who controls it? Who benefits from it? Until these questions are answered, no other reform will be effective. Land policy is not one policy among many. It is the policy that makes all other policies possible.

If you want to understand a society, ask: who owns the land?

土地政策是治理的基礎

所有治理正當性的根源
GFI · 土地與住房系列

所有治理正當性最終都歸結於一個問題：誰擁有土地？答案決定了誰擁有權力、誰承擔成本、誰從系統中受益。

一、為什麼是土地？

土地不只是另一種資產。它是所有財富的基礎。一切都在土地上建造：住房、工業、農業、基礎設施。沒有土地，就沒有經濟。沒有對土地的控制，就沒有對權力的控制。

縱觀歷史，統治階級一直是土地擁有階級。封建領主擁有土地。殖民帝國擁有土地。現代寡頭擁有土地。

誰控制土地，誰就控制社會。

二、土地如何決定治理

土地制度誰受益誰承擔成本治理結果
公有制人民國家（投資）穩定、公平、長期
私有制（管制）地主與社會消費者（租戶、買家）不穩定但可控
私有制（無管制）地主所有人不穩定、不公平、易危機
企業所有權股東勞工、租戶、納稅人掠奪性、剝削性

三、GL框架視角

GL 維度土地政策如何塑造治理
成本轉嫁私有土地所有制將住房、基礎設施和發展的成本轉嫁給公眾
轉移路徑土地價值向上流向地主，而非回到社區
資訊不對稱土地所有權常被隱藏；公眾不知道誰掌握權力
閉環程度地主形成一個封閉階級，利用其權力保護土地所有權
等待成本投機使土地空置，而人們等待住房；等待的成本由無家可歸者和年輕人承擔

四、中國 vs. 西方：兩種模式

  • 中國：土地是公共資產。國家分配土地用於發展。投機被控制。住房是用來住的。
  • 西方：土地是私有商品。土地被交易、投機和囤積。住房是利潤中心。

差異不是經濟性的。它是根本性的——它被寫入系統的基礎。

五、結論：土地政策是核心

每個治理問題最終都會回到土地。誰擁有它？誰控制它？誰從中受益？在這些問題被回答之前，沒有其他改革會有效。土地政策不是眾多政策之一。它是使所有其他政策成為可能的政策。

如果你想了解一個社會，問：誰擁有土地？
