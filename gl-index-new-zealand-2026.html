Key Findings

  • Transparency — One of the most transparent governments in the world. The Official Information Act sets the standard.
  • Māori Participation — Bicultural governance arrangements (Te Tiriti o Waitangi) are a unique form of institutional inclusion.
  • Social Trust — High trust in institutions. The system works because citizens believe it works.
  • Economic Isolation — Small economy, geographically isolated. Infrastructure is aging.
  • Housing Affordability — Major weakness. Housing prices have risen rapidly in recent years.
  • Indigenous Governance — The Māori partnership is a work in progress. Full inclusion is not yet complete.

What 0.70 Means

New Zealand is one of the most transparent countries in the world. Its bicultural governance model is a unique experiment in institutional inclusion. But the economy is small, infrastructure is aging, and housing affordability is a growing crisis. The model is good — but it is not robust.
