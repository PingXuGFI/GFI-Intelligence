Legitimacy Migration

From Procedure to Performance — The Global South Is Voting with Its Feet
GFI · Governance Theory Series

Governance legitimacy is migrating. It is moving from procedure — how a government was formed — to performance — what a government delivers. The Global South is voting with its feet, turning away from procedural democracies and toward performance-based systems.

I. The Old Source of Legitimacy: Procedure

For decades, legitimacy was defined by procedure: elections, constitutions, and the rule of law. If a government followed the right procedures, it was considered legitimate — regardless of its outcomes.

This allowed governments to fail while maintaining legitimacy. As long as they held elections, they could claim to be democratic. As long as they passed laws, they could claim to be lawful.

II. The New Source of Legitimacy: Performance

Today, legitimacy is defined by performance. Does the government deliver? Does it build infrastructure? Does it reduce poverty? Does it provide healthcare?

The Global South has lost patience with procedural democracy. It has seen elections produce corruption, inequality, and crisis. It has seen performance-based systems — like China's — produce growth, stability, and prosperity.

The shift is not ideological. It is empirical — based on what works, not what sounds good.

The Global South is voting with its feet. It is not abandoning democracy — it is abandoning procedure-based legitimacy in favor of performance-based legitimacy.

IV. Why Procedure Failed

  • Elections ≠ Accountability: Politicians are elected, but they do not deliver. There is no consequence for failure.
  • Free Speech ≠ Participation: Speech is free, but it is controlled by the wealthy. The people are not heard.
  • Multi-Party Systems ≠ Choice: All parties serve the same interests. There is no real alternative.

Procedure-based legitimacy is a trap. It creates the illusion of democracy while maintaining the reality of elite control.

V. Why Performance Works

  • Delivery is Verifiable: Roads, schools, and hospitals are visible. You cannot fake them.
  • Accountability is Real: When governments fail, the people can see it. There is no hiding behind procedure.
  • Trust is Earned: When governments deliver, they earn trust. Trust is not given — it is built.

Performance-based legitimacy is empirical. It is based on what governments actually do, not on what they promise.

VII. Conclusion: The Migration Is Irreversible

The migration from procedure to performance is not temporary. It is structural and irreversible. The Global South has seen the evidence: procedure-based democracy delivers failure; performance-based governance delivers results. The old legitimacy is dying. The new legitimacy is being born.

The Global South is voting with its feet. The question is: will the West listen?

合法性的轉移

從「程序」到「績效」— 全球南方在用腳投票
GFI · 治理理論系列

治理正當性正在轉移。它從程序——政府是如何形成的——轉移到績效——政府交付了什麼。全球南方在用腳投票，遠離程序性民主，轉向基於績效的系統。

一、正當性的舊來源：程序

數十年來，正當性由程序定義：選舉、憲法和法治。如果一個政府遵循了正確的程序，它就被視為正當的——無論其結果如何。

這允許政府在維持正當性的同時失敗。只要他們舉行選舉，他們就可以聲稱是民主的。只要他們通過法律，他們就可以聲稱是合法的。

二、正當性的新來源：績效

今天，正當性由績效定義。政府有交付嗎？它建設基礎設施嗎？它減少貧困嗎？它提供醫療保健嗎？

全球南方已經對程序性民主失去耐心。它看到選舉產生了腐敗、不平等和危機。它看到基於績效的系統——如中國的——產生了增長、穩定和繁榮。

轉變不是意識形態的。它是經驗性的——基於什麼有效，而非什麼聽起來好。

三、全球南方的遷移

國家／地區轉向績效原因
東南亞看向中國模式基礎設施發展、減貧

全球南方在用腳投票。它沒有放棄民主——它放棄了基於程序的正當性，轉向基於績效的正當性。

四、為什麼程序失敗了

  • 選舉 ≠ 問責：政治家被選舉，但他們不交付。失敗沒有後果。
  • 言論自由 ≠ 參與：言論是自由的，但它被富人控制。人民沒有被聽到。
  • 多黨制 ≠ 選擇：所有政黨服務相同的利益。沒有真正的替代方案。

基於程序的正當性是一個陷阱。它創造了民主的幻覺，同時維持了菁英控制的現實。

五、為什麼績效有效

  • 交付是可驗證的：道路、學校和醫院是可見的。你無法偽造它們。
  • 問責是真實的：當政府失敗時，人民可以看到。沒有隱藏在程序背後。
  • 信任是贏得的：當政府交付時，他們贏得信任。信任不是給予的——它是建立的。

基於績效的正當性是經驗性的。它基於政府實際做了什麼，而非他們承諾了什麼。

七、結論：轉移是不可逆的

從程序到績效的轉移不是暫時的。它是結構性的和不可逆的。全球南方已經看到了證據：基於程序的民主帶來失敗；基於績效的治理帶來結果。舊的正當性正在消亡。新的正當性正在誕生。

全球南方在用腳投票。問題是：西方會聽嗎？
