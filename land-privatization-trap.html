Land Privatization:
The Century-Long Nightmare for 98% of the Propertyless

Why private land ownership is a trap for the vast majority – and why public ownership is the only way out

I. Land Is Not a Commodity – It Is the Lifeline of Existence

In a 2025 report to the UN General Assembly, the Special Rapporteur on adequate housing, Balakrishnan Rajagopal, warned unequivocally: “The global housing crisis cannot be solved without addressing land inequality and land speculation.” The report stressed that land is increasingly treated as a commodity, prices skyrocket, speculation eats away affordability, and millions face forced evictions, displacement, and dispossession.

Land is not ordinary property to be bought and sold. “It is where people root themselves, express their culture, build communities, and create a sense of belonging.” When land is commodified, monopolised by a few, and treated as a speculative asset, the 98% who own no land are condemned to paying with their lives and labour for the mere right to shelter.

II. Land Privatization: A Legalised Mechanism of Plunder

Mainstream propaganda tells you that privatising land increases supply and lowers house prices. That is a lie.

In cities like Mumbai, where land is privately owned, housing is anything but affordable. In the United States, Japan, Taiwan, and other places where private land ownership dominates, cities cannot build dense high‑rise housing efficiently; housing sprawls over the land, making land use inefficient. Private ownership does not lower prices – it creates far more devastating problems:

  • First, private land ownership allows a tiny minority to monopolise the living space of everyone else. Land is finite. When land can be privately owned, that means a few can legally claim the vast majority of land resources, while the rest must rent or buy the right to exist from them. This is not a free market – it is legalised feudalism.
  • Second, private land ownership turns land into a vehicle for speculation, not habitation. Under private ownership, land is not for living – it is for flipping. The rich buy land and wait for appreciation; developers hoard land to drive up prices; financial capital packages land as an investment product. When land becomes a tool for wealth accumulation, housing becomes a luxury.
  • Third, private land ownership creates a permanent wealth chasm. Studies show that the rich can pay for the location amenities they desire, thereby bidding up land prices. High land prices exclude low‑income groups from affordable housing. You rent – your rent goes to the landlord, the landowner. You buy – most of the price is the land value, which still goes to the landowner. Your lifetime of labour flows inexorably into the pockets of landowners. You are always working for someone else’s land.

III. Houses Depreciate, Land Does Not – The Fatal Deception

Mainstream propaganda wants you to believe that “owning a house means owning property.” This is the greatest deception.

A house is a structure on the ground – a building. It depreciates, ages, and loses value. The millions you pay today will be worth a fraction of that in thirty years. The only thing that truly appreciates, that always increases in value, is the land beneath it.

But under private land ownership, buying a house does not mean you own the land. You merely buy ownership of a depreciating structure, while the land – the real asset that appreciates forever – remains in the hands of a few. You think you are building wealth, but in reality you are merely paying for the landowner’s asset appreciation.

This is a carefully designed wealth transfer mechanism: 98% of people spend a lifetime of savings and thirty years of debt to buy a depreciating shell, while simultaneously contributing to the ever‑increasing land value of the 1% who own the land.

IV. Public Land Ownership: The Only Way Out

China practices public land ownership – urban land is state‑owned, rural land is collectively owned. This means land belongs to no private individual, cannot be monopolised, cannot be speculated upon, and cannot be treated as a speculative commodity.

Research has proven that public land ownership is a systemic advantage of China, and privatisation is not viable. Under public ownership, ownership is separated from use rights, allowing market‑based allocation to solve efficiency problems. Moreover, studies on allowing rural collective land to enter the market show that the wealth Gini coefficient could drop by 16%, significantly reducing wealth inequality.

Public land ownership guarantees equality of starting point – the basic land needs of every citizen are not monopolised by a few. It enables the government to regulate land supply, curb speculation, and return housing to its essential purpose – living – rather than allowing it to become a tool for the few to plunder the many.

V. To the 98% of You

You own no land. You will never own land. Under private land ownership, the harder you work, the higher land prices go; the higher they go, the less you can afford; the less you can afford, the more you have to rent; the more you rent, the more your landlord can buy even more land.

This is a closed loop of exploitation. You are trapped inside it, and no amount of effort will break you out.

Private land ownership has never been an opportunity for the 98% – it has always been a shackle. It makes you believe the free market is fair, when in reality it allows a few to legally claim everyone else’s living space. It makes you believe buying a house is accumulating wealth, when in reality it makes you spend your entire life’s labour enriching landowners.

Land should belong to everyone, not to a few. Land should be for living, not for speculation. Land should be the foundation of rights, not the instrument of exploitation.

This is the moment for the 98% to wake up. Do not be deceived by the lie that “private land equals freedom.” Private land ownership is never freedom – it is the legalised plunder of the many by the few. Public land ownership is the only path forward for the 98%.

土地私有：
98%無產階級的世紀夢魘

——為什麼土地私有是絕大多數人的陷阱，而土地公有是唯一的出路

一、土地不是商品，是生存的命脈

聯合國適當住房權問題特別報告員巴拉克里希南·拉賈格帕爾在2025年向聯合國大會提交的報告中明確警告：「全球住房危機若不解決土地不平等、投機問題，就無法得到解決。」報告指出，全球土地日益被視為商品，價格飆升、投機行為侵蝕可負擔性，數百萬人面臨強迫驅逐、流離失所和剝奪。

土地不是可以買賣的普通財產。「它是人們紮根、表達文化、建立社區和歸屬感的地方。」當土地被視為商品，被少數人壟斷、炒作、囤積，98%沒有土地所有權的人，就注定要為棲身之所付出世代的血汗。

二、土地私有：合法化的掠奪機制

主流宣傳告訴你：土地私有化能增加供應、降低房價。這是謊言。

在孟買這樣土地私有的城市，房價絲毫不便宜。在美國、日本、台灣等土地私有為主的地區，城市無法建立密集的高樓大廈，房屋平攤在土地上，土地利用效率低下。土地私有不僅沒有降低房價，反而製造了更嚴重的問題：

  • 第一，土地私有讓少數人壟斷了所有人的生存空間。土地是有限的。當土地可以私有，就意味著少數人可以合法地佔有絕大部分土地資源，而多數人只能向他們租賃或購買生存的權利。這不是自由市場，這是合法化的封建莊園制。
  • 第二，土地私有讓投機取代了居住。在土地私有制下，土地不是用來住的，是用來炒的。富人購置土地等待升值，開發商囤積土地推高房價，金融資本將土地包裝成投資產品。當土地成為財富積累的工具，居住就成了一種奢侈品。
  • 第三，土地私有製造了永久的財富鴻溝。研究顯示，富人擁有資源支付他們想要的區位屬性，從而推高土地價格。高地價將低收入人群排除在可負擔的居住之外。你租房，租金付給房東——地主。你買房，房價的大部分是地價——還是付給地主。你一生勞動，財富源源不絕流向土地所有者。你永遠在為別人的土地打工。

三、房子會貶值，土地不會——這是致命的騙局

主流宣傳讓你以為「有房就是有產」。這是最大的欺騙。

房子是地上物，是建築物，它會折舊、會老化、會貶值。你今天花幾百萬買的房子，三十年後建築本身價值所剩無幾。真正值錢的、永遠增值的，是腳下的那塊土地。

但在土地私有制下，你買了房子，不等於你擁有土地。你只是買了一個地上物的所有權，而土地的所有權——那個真正會增值的東西——掌握在少數人手中。你以為自己在累積財富，實際上你只是在替土地所有者支付他們的資產增值。

這是一個精心設計的財富轉移機制：98%的人用一生的儲蓄和三十年的負債，購買一個會貶值的殼，同時為1%的土地所有者貢獻了永恆增值的地價。

四、土地公有：唯一的出路

中國實行土地公有制——城市土地國有、農村土地集體所有。這意味著土地不屬於任何私人，不能被壟斷、不能被炒作、不能被當作投機商品。

研究證明，土地公有制是中國的制度優勢，土地私有化不可取。在土地公有制下，土地所有權與使用權分離，實行市場化配置，解決了效率問題。而允許農村集體土地入市的研究更顯示，財富基尼係數可以下降16%，顯著降低財富不平等。

土地公有制保障了起點公平——每個公民的基本用地需求不被少數人壟斷。它讓政府能夠調控土地供給，抑制投機，讓房子回歸居住的本質，而不是成為少數人掠奪多數人的工具。

五、給98%的你

你沒有土地。你永遠不會有土地。在土地私有制下，你越努力工作，地價越高；地價越高，你越買不起；你越買不起，越要租房；你越租房，房東越有錢去買更多土地。

這是一個封閉的剝削循環。你身在其中，永遠不可能靠努力打破它。

土地私有對98%的人從來不是機會，而是枷鎖。它讓你以為自由市場是公平的，實際上它讓少數人合法地佔有所有人的生存空間。它讓你以為買房就是累積財富，實際上它讓你用一生的勞動為地主創造財富。

土地應該屬於所有人，而不是少數人。土地應該用來居住，而不是用來投機。土地應該是權利的基礎，而不是剝削的工具。

這是98%的人必須覺醒的時刻。不要再被「土地私有等於自由」的謊言欺騙。土地私有從來不是自由，它是少數人對多數人的合法掠奪。土地公有，才是98%的人唯一的出路。
