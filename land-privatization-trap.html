Land Privatization: The Century-Long Nightmare

Why Private Land Ownership Is a Trap for the Vast Majority
GFI · Land & Housing Series

Private land ownership is not freedom. It is a trap — designed to enrich a small minority at the expense of the vast majority. The evidence is overwhelming: wherever land has been privatized, inequality rises, housing becomes unaffordable, and the working class is dispossessed.

I. The History of Enclosure

The privatization of land is not a natural process. It is a historical project — carried out through force, law, and ideology. The Enclosure Acts in England dispossessed millions of peasants. Colonial land seizures dispossessed entire continents. Neoliberal privatization is the latest phase.

Each phase has the same logic: take land from the many, give it to the few, and call it "property rights."

II. How the Trap Works

StageWhat HappensWho BenefitsWho Loses
1. PrivatizationPublic land is sold to private ownersElites, corporationsThe public
2. SpeculationLand prices rise as owners wait for profitLandownersEveryone else
3. DispossessionPeople who cannot afford housing are displacedNo one (renters lose)Working class, poor, young
4. CrisisHousing bubble bursts, economy collapsesBankers, bailout recipientsEveryone else

The trap is not a failure of the system. It is the system working as designed — transferring wealth from the many to the few.

The correlation is clear: more privatization = more inequality = more unaffordable housing.

IV. The Ideological Trap

Why do people accept this system? Because they are told that private property is the foundation of freedom. But freedom for whom? For the landowner who can charge any rent? For the speculator who can buy and sell land without producing anything?

Private land ownership is not freedom. It is feudalism with a modern label.

V. GL Framework Diagnosis

GL DimensionHow the Trap Works
Information AsymmetryThe public is told that privatization creates prosperity — while the data shows the opposite
Cost ShiftingThe costs of land privatization are shifted onto the poor through higher rents and displacement
Transfer PathwaysLand value flows upward to landowners, not back to the community
Psychological CostThe public is made to feel that they are "failing" if they cannot afford housing
Closure IntensityThe landowning class uses its power to protect privatization — through lobbying, media, and politics

VI. Conclusion: The Trap Must Be Escaped

Land privatization is not a natural or inevitable system. It is a choice — made by the powerful, for the powerful. It is a trap for the majority. The only way to escape is to reclaim land for the public — to end the commodification of land and restore it as a common good.

The trap is not a failure. It is a design. And designs can be changed.

土地私有化：世紀噩夢

為什麼私有土地所有權對絕大多數人是陷阱
GFI · 土地與住房系列

私有土地所有權不是自由。它是一個陷阱——設計來讓少數人致富，以犧牲絕大多數人為代價。證據是壓倒性的：無論何處土地被私有化，不平等就會上升，住房變得不可負擔，勞工階級被剝奪。

一、圈地的歷史

土地私有化不是自然過程。它是一個歷史計畫——通過武力、法律和意識形態執行。英國的圈地法令剝奪了數百萬農民。殖民土地掠奪剝奪了整個大陸。新自由主義私有化是最新階段。

每個階段都有相同的邏輯：從多數人手中奪走土地，給予少數人，並稱之為「產權」。

二、陷阱如何運作

階段發生什麼誰受益誰受害
1. 私有化公共土地被出售給私人所有者菁英、企業公眾
2. 投機地主等待利潤，土地價格上漲地主所有人
3. 剝奪無法負擔住房的人被驅逐無人（租戶受害）勞工階級、窮人、年輕人
4. 危機房地產泡沫破裂，經濟崩潰銀行家、紓困受益者所有人

陷阱不是系統的失敗。它是系統按照設計運作——將財富從多數人轉移到少數人。

三、數據：私有化導致不平等

相關性很清楚：更多私有化 = 更多不平等 = 更不可負擔的住房。

四、意識形態陷阱

為什麼人們接受這個系統？因為他們被告知私有財產是自由的基礎。但對誰的自由？對可以收取任何租金的地主？對可以不生產任何東西就買賣土地的投機者？

私有土地所有權不是自由。它是帶有現代標籤的封建主義。

五、GL框架診斷

六、結論：必須逃離陷阱

土地私有化不是自然或不可避免的系統。它是一個選擇——由有權者為有權者所做。它是對多數人的陷阱。逃離的唯一方式是為公眾收回土地——終結土地的商品化，恢復其為公共財。

陷阱不是失敗。它是設計。而設計可以被改變。
