Germany scores 0.55 on the GL Index — a C grade. It is the best-performing country in the C tier, but it is still only halfway to optimal governance. Germany has strong institutions and a federal system that provides redundancy and local accountability. But it also has deep structural problems.

Key Findings

  • Strong Federalism — Local accountability and redundancy protect against systemic collapse, but create fragmentation.
  • Digitalization Lag — Germany lags behind other developed countries in digital government. Broadband and digital ID are uneven.
  • Energy Policy — The shift away from nuclear and coal created costs that were not fully anticipated.
  • Bureaucratic Rigidity — Strong rule of law creates procedural complexity. Approval times are long.
  • Housing Crisis — Major cities like Berlin and Munich have rapidly rising housing costs.
  • Infrastructure Failure — Rail, bridges, and digital infrastructure are deteriorating after decades of underinvestment.

What 0.55 Means

Germany is the best-performing country in the C tier. It has strong institutions, but they are increasingly inefficient. The federal system provides redundancy, but also fragmentation. Digitalization lag and infrastructure neglect are eroding governance performance.
