蕉嶺三圳的土壤密碼

解開世界長壽鄉的「地」一桶金
GFI Wellness · 2026年9月

在廣東省東北部的蕉嶺縣三圳鎮，有一片看似平凡卻極不平凡的土壤。這裡的人們世代耕作，種出的淮山肥美、稻米香甜，而這片土地下埋藏著的，可能是當地人健康長壽的關鍵密碼——硒。

一、富硒土壤：大自然的饋贈

根據官方檢測數據，蕉嶺全縣土壤的平均含硒量為每公斤0.613毫克，最高達0.93毫克。而國家富硒標準為土壤含硒量每公斤0.4毫克以上，蕉嶺的平均值比國家標準高出75%。

更值得注意的是，在蕉嶺全縣97個村的採樣檢測中，69.6%的樣點土壤硒含量超過了國家富硒標準。這意味著——這片土地不是零星有硒，而是全域富硒。

三圳鎮位於石窟河與柚樹河交匯處，地勢平坦，以平原和丘陵為主，土壤肥沃、水源充足，歷來是蕉嶺的「魚米之鄉」。而這裡的土壤不僅肥沃，更因為富硒而獨一無二。

這片土地的硒含量是國家標準的1.75倍。全域富硒，不是偶然，是億萬年的地質饋贈。

二、「七沙三土」：三圳淮山的秘密

三圳鎮的土壤有一個特別的名稱——「七沙三土」。這是一種沙質與黏土比例約7:3的特殊土質，透氣性好、排水性佳，非常適合根莖類作物的生長。

這裡的淮山種植戶有30多年的經驗，他們告訴記者：「因土質特別，地下水較為均勻，用三鳥糞便做肥料，種下去長大後就施用豆麩。」

這種傳統耕作方式與富硒土壤相結合，種出的三圳淮山具有以下特點：

特點說明
肥大粗壯沙土鬆軟，利於淮山向下生長
根鬚少土質均勻，養分充足
肉色潔白澱粉和膠質含量高
口感松軟香滑深受消費者喜愛

三圳淮山已被評為國家地理標誌保護產品，同時也是蕉嶺十大長壽食品之一。

三、從土壤到餐桌：完整的富硒食物鏈

蕉嶺的富硒土壤，不僅僅是「土壤有硒」這麼簡單。它形成了一條完整的食物鏈：

富硒土壤 → 富硒農作物 → 富硒食物 → 人體吸收 → 健康長壽

蕉嶺縣將長壽食品產業歸納為「五個一」：一杯茶、一瓶水、一粒米、一棵筍、一瓶蜜。這「五個一」，全部來自這片富硒土地的饋贈。

四、科學背書：省級研究機構進駐

富硒土壤的價值，不僅農民知道，科學家也知道。

廣東省科學院已在蕉嶺設立了「富硒生物科技創新中心」，為蕉嶺富硒農業產業發展提供技術支撐。這意味著蕉嶺的富硒土壤已經進入了系統性科學研究的階段。

截至目前，蕉嶺已有37個富硒生產基地被評為市級「富硒土壤種植示範基地」，數量居全市第一。這些基地種植的農產品，從源頭上保證了硒元素的含量。

五、為什麼硒與長壽有關？

硒是人體必需的微量元素，具有以下功能：

  • 抗氧化：硒是穀胱甘肽過氧化物酶的重要組成部分，能清除體內自由基
  • 延緩衰老：通過抗氧化作用減少細胞損傷
  • 增強免疫功能：促進免疫細胞的活性
  • 保護心血管：降低氧化應激對血管的損傷

國際自然医学会會長森下敬一在授予蕉嶺「世界長壽鄉」牌證時說：「蕉嶺長壽鄉是一個純天然的能量場，去這個地方靜養休息，會得到金錢無法買到的『氣』。」而這個「能量場」的核心，就是這片富硒的土地。

這片富硒土地之所以能養育一代又一代的長壽老人，有一個根本原因——這片土地從未被私有化。

✅ 蕉嶺（土地公有）

蕉嶺的長壽不是來自土地私有，而是恰恰相反——來自土地不被私有化。土地公有，是長壽現象得以延續的根本前提。

這片土地不只是蕉嶺人的土地，它是人類與自然和諧共處的活教材。

在這裡，土壤的營養沒有被資本化，而是被轉化為一代又一代人的健康與長壽。這種模式，值得全世界學習。

The Soil Code of Jiaoling's Sanzhen Town

Unlocking the "Ground" Gold of the World's Longevity Township
GFI Wellness · September 2026

In Sanzhen Town, Jiaoling County, northeastern Guangdong Province, there lies a seemingly ordinary yet extraordinary soil. For generations, people here have cultivated yams and rice that are plump and fragrant. Beneath this land lies the key to the locals' health and longevity — selenium.

I. Selenium-Rich Soil: A Gift of Nature

Official test data shows that the average selenium content of Jiaoling's soil is 0.613 mg/kg, with a maximum of 0.93 mg/kg. China's national standard for selenium-rich soil is 0.4 mg/kg — Jiaoling's average exceeds the national standard by 75%.

More notably, among samples taken from 97 villages across Jiaoling County, 69.6% exceeded the national selenium-rich standard. This means — this land is not sporadically rich in selenium, but selenium-rich across its entire area.

Sanzhen Town sits at the confluence of the Shiku River and the Youshu River. Its flat terrain, fertile soil, and abundant water sources have long made it the "land of fish and rice" of Jiaoling. And this soil is not only fertile — it is uniquely selenium-rich.

This land's selenium content is 1.75 times the national standard. Across-the-board selenium richness is not accidental — it is a geological gift of millions of years.

II. "Seven Parts Sand, Three Parts Clay": The Secret of Sanzhen Yam

Sanzhen's soil has a special name — "Seven Parts Sand, Three Parts Clay". This unique soil composition, with a sand-to-clay ratio of about 7:3, offers excellent aeration and drainage, making it ideal for root crops.

Yam farmers here, with over 30 years of experience, explain: "The soil is special, and the groundwater is evenly distributed. We use poultry manure as fertilizer, and after the yams grow, we apply bean pulp."

This traditional farming method, combined with selenium-rich soil, produces Sanzhen yams with the following characteristics:

Soft and Smooth TasteHighly favored by consumers

Sanzhen yam has been recognized as a National Geographical Indication Product and is also one of Jiaoling's Top Ten Longevity Foods.

IV. Scientific Backing: Provincial Research Institution Established

The value of selenium-rich soil is recognized not only by farmers but also by scientists.

The Guangdong Academy of Sciences has established the "Selenium-Rich Biotechnology Innovation Center" in Jiaoling, providing technical support for the development of Jiaoling's selenium-rich agriculture. This means Jiaoling's selenium-rich soil has entered the stage of systematic scientific research.

To date, Jiaoling has 37 selenium-rich production bases certified as municipal-level "Selenium-Rich Soil Planting Demonstration Bases," ranking first in the city. The agricultural products from these bases guarantee selenium content from the source.

V. Why Is Selenium Linked to Longevity?

Selenium is an essential trace mineral for the human body with the following functions:

  • Antioxidant: Selenium is a key component of glutathione peroxidase, eliminating free radicals
  • Anti-aging: Reduces cellular damage through antioxidant effects
  • Immune enhancement: Promotes immune cell activity
  • Cardiovascular protection: Reduces oxidative stress damage to blood vessels

Dr. Kenji Morishita, President of the International Natural Medicine Association, said when awarding Jiaoling the "World Longevity Township" certification: "Jiaoling is a pure natural energy field. Resting and recovering here will give you something money cannot buy — 'Qi'." And the core of this "energy field" is this selenium-rich land.
