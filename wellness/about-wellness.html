What We Care About

GFI Wellness is built on the foundation of Blue Zones research — exploring how humans can return to nature — living in harmony with the environment, staying connected to family, and supporting each other in community — to live long and live well.

We are particularly focused on the longevity phenomenon of Jiaoling County, Guangdong Province, China — especially Jiuling Village in Sanzhen Town — and are committed to advocating for its official recognition as a Blue Zone.
