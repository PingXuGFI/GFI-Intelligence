UK & US: Neoliberalism's Governance Collapse

A GL Framework Diagnosis — Why This Diagnosis Is Credible
GFI · September 2026

The United Kingdom and the United States — the birthplace and primary drivers of neoliberalism — present, under the GL Framework's diagnosis, a systematic collapse of governance legitimacy. Their GDP figures may be high, but their people are paying the price: deteriorating quality of life, declining health indicators, and crumbling social trust.

I. GL Framework Diagnostic Criteria

GL DimensionWhat We Are Measuring
Information TransparencyCan the people access complete information? Is the media independent or controlled?
Cost ShiftingWho bears the costs of governance failures — the powerful or the vulnerable?
Structural IntegrityDoes the government have the capacity to serve the people effectively?
Outcome VerifiabilityAre the people's quality of life, health, and longevity improving?
Feedback MechanismCan the people change policy direction through the system?
