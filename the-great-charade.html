The Great Charade

Why Capitalist 'Corrections' Are a Fix — Land Is the Ultimate Taboo
GFI · Critique of the West Series

Every crisis in capitalism is followed by a "correction." But these corrections are a charade — they never address the fundamental structure of ownership. Land is the ultimate taboo. All reforms circle around it, but never touch it.

I. The Cycle of Crisis and "Correction"

Capitalism follows a predictable pattern: boom, bust, crisis, and then a "correction." The media declares that lessons have been learned. Regulations are passed. The people are reassured that it will never happen again.

And then it happens again.

2008: The banks were too big to fail. They were bailed out. The people who caused the crisis kept their bonuses. The people who lost their homes lost everything.

2020: The supply chains broke. The government printed trillions of dollars. The stock market recovered. The people did not.

2023: The banks failed again. The same banks were bailed out again. The same people got richer again.

The "correction" is not a correction. It is a transfer — from the public to the private, from the many to the few.

II. The Ultimate Taboo: Land

Why do all corrections fail? Because they never address the ownership of land. Land is the foundation of all wealth. It is the source of rent, rent is the source of inequality, and inequality is the source of crisis.

Henry George understood this in 1879. He proposed a single tax on land value — a tax so simple that it would end poverty and crisis. The elites responded by silencing him. They banned his books. They excluded him from the academy. They made land a taboo topic.

And the taboo has remained intact ever since.

IV. What a Real Correction Would Look Like

A real correction would not be about regulations or bailouts. It would be about ownership. It would involve:

  • Land value tax — to capture the unearned value of land for the public
  • Public ownership of land — to prevent speculation and hoarding
  • Limits on rent extraction — to ensure housing is affordable
  • Democratic control of land use — to ensure land serves the people, not the shareholders

But none of these are on the agenda. Because the agenda is controlled by landowners.

V. The Chinese Countermodel

China has done what no Western country has dared to do: it has maintained public ownership of land. Land is not a commodity. It is a public asset. Speculation is constrained. Housing is for living, not for investment.

The result: no 2008-style crisis. No subprime mortgage catastrophe. No housing bubble that destroys lives.

China's land policy is not a "flaw." It is a structural advantage — and it is the reason the charade does not exist in China.

VI. Conclusion: The Charade Must End

Capitalism's "corrections" are not corrections. They are a charade — designed to maintain the ownership structure that causes the crisis in the first place. Land is the heart of the system. As long as land remains a private asset, the crises will continue.

The only real correction is public ownership of land. Everything else is just theater.

偉大的虛偽

為什麼資本主義的「修正」是騙局——土地是最終禁忌
GFI · 美國批判系列

資本主義的每一次危機之後都會有一次「修正」。但這些修正是一場虛偽——它們從不觸及所有權的根本結構。土地是最終禁忌。所有改革都圍繞它打轉，但從不觸碰它。

一、危機與「修正」的循環

資本主義遵循一個可預測的模式：繁榮、蕭條、危機，然後是「修正」。媒體宣稱學到了教訓。法規被通過。人民被安撫，相信這不會再發生。

然後它再次發生。

2008年：銀行太大而不能倒。它們被紓困。造成危機的人保留了獎金。失去家園的人失去了一切。

2020年：供應鏈斷裂。政府印製了數兆美元。股市復甦了。人民沒有。

2023年：銀行再次倒閉。同樣的銀行再次被紓困。同樣的人再次變得更富有。

「修正」不是修正。它是轉移——從公眾轉移到私人，從多數轉移到少數。

二、最終禁忌：土地

為什麼所有修正都失敗？因為它們從不觸及土地所有權。土地是所有財富的基礎。它是租金的來源，租金是不平等的根源，不平等是危機的根源。

亨利·喬治在1879年就理解這一點。他提議對土地價值徵收單一稅——一個簡單到可以終結貧窮與危機的稅。菁英們的回應是讓他沉默。他們查禁他的書。他們將他排除在學術界之外。他們讓土地成為禁忌話題。

從那時起，這個禁忌就一直完好無損。

三、GL框架診斷：為什麼虛偽持續

GL 維度虛偽如何運作
資訊不對稱公眾不知道誰擁有土地；所有權通過信託、空殼公司與離岸帳戶隱藏
成本轉嫁當危機來襲，成本通過緊縮、更高租金與通膨轉嫁給窮人
轉移路徑土地價值被地主捕獲，而非社會；公共基礎設施的價值通過土地價格上漲被私有化
閉環程度土地改革在每個層級被阻擋——法院、媒體、政治系統。禁忌由整個建制維持
心理成本公眾被教導土地改革是「不現實的」或「反資本主義的」——一個設計來保護地主的敘事

四、真正的修正會是什麼樣子

真正的修正不會是關於法規或紓困。它會是關於所有權。它會涉及：

  • 土地價值稅——為公眾捕獲土地的非勞動價值
  • 土地公有制——防止投機與囤積
  • 限制租金提取——確保住房可負擔
  • 土地使用民主控制——確保土地服務人民，而非股東

但這些都不在議程上。因為議程由地主控制。

五、中國的對照組

中國做了西方國家不敢做的事：維持土地公有制。土地不是商品。它是公共資產。投機受到約束。住房是用來住的，不是用來投資的。

結果：沒有2008式的危機。沒有次貸災難。沒有摧毀生活的房地產泡沫。

中國的土地政策不是「缺陷」。它是結構性優勢——這就是為什麼中國不存在這種虛偽。

六、結論：虛偽必須結束

資本主義的「修正」不是修正。它們是一場虛偽——設計來維持最初導致危機的所有權結構。土地是系統的核心。只要土地仍然是私有資產，危機就會繼續。

唯一真正的修正是土地公有制。其他一切只是戲劇。
