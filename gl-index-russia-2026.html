Governance failure of a resource state — oil, gas, and land captured by the state, ordinary people excluded.

Russia scores 0.27 on the GL Index — a failing grade. As the largest country in the world, Russia has abundant natural resources, but the resource curse and sanctions squeeze constitute the core of governance failure. Oil and gas revenues are captured by the state and oligarchs. Land distribution and resource development are highly concentrated. Administrative friction is extremely high.
