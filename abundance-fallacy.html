The Abundance Fallacy

Why Elon Musk's future cannot happen under Western liberal economics

GL Framework Structural Diagnosis 8 Parameters

Elon Musk paints a future of radical abundance: unlimited solar energy, AI replacing all labor, universal basic income, and humanity colonizing Mars. As an engineering vision, it is plausible. As an economic forecast, it is a fantasy — not because the technology is impossible, but because Western liberal economics is structurally incompatible with abundance.

How Western liberal economics actively blocks the abundance future — parameter by parameter.

1. Waiting Cost Regulatory delays, permitting battles, and litigation stall solar farms, transmission lines, and nuclear plants for 10–20 years. Abundance cannot wait.
2. Repetition Cost Competing proprietary standards (charging plugs, battery formats, OS ecosystems) force redundant investment. Every company reinvents the wheel — and charges for it.
3. Collapse Cost No resilience mechanism. When supply chains break or climate disasters hit, the market has no contingency — only bailouts for the rich.
4. Information Asymmetry Critical energy and AI data are held by private monopolies. Citizens, regulators, and even governments operate in the dark.
5. Psychological Cost Job insecurity, gig-economy precarity, and debt-driven anxiety are structural features, not bugs. A terrified population does not demand abundance — it clings to survival.
6. Opportunity Cost The future that never happens: fusion research underfunded, public transit abandoned, desalination ignored — because the payback period is too long for Wall Street.
7. Transfer Pathways Publicly funded R&D (DARPA, NIH, NASA) is privatized via exclusive licensing. Socialized risk, privatized reward — abundance's value flows upward, not outward.
8. Closure Intensity Lobbying, campaign finance, and revolving-door appointments ensure that any policy threatening incumbent profits is killed before it reaches a vote.

The Chinese Countermodel: Planning for Abundance

China does not wait for the market to deliver abundance. It builds it: Ultra-High-Voltage grids, solar & battery dominance, high-speed rail, and a state-directed space program. These are not "market outcomes." They are institutional choices made by a system that can tolerate losses, delay profits, and think in decades.

Conclusion: Abundance Must Be Planned, Not Prayed For

The future Musk describes will either not happen at all, or it will happen under a different institutional architecture — one capable of long-range coordination, public ownership of infrastructure, and a political economy not beholden to quarterly earnings. That architecture exists. It is called China's socialist market economy.

豐盛的謬誤

為什麼 Elon Musk 的未來不可能發生在西方自由主義經濟下

GL 框架 結構性診斷 八大參數

伊隆·馬斯克描繪的未來是極度豐盛的：無限太陽能、AI取代所有勞動、全民基本收入、人類殖民火星。作為工程願景，它在物理上是可行的；作為經濟預測，它卻是幻想——不是因為技術做不到，而是因為西方自由主義經濟在結構上與豐盛無法相容。

西方自由主義經濟如何逐項阻礙豐盛未來——逐一參數檢視。

1. 等待成本 法規延誤、許可證訴訟，使太陽能電廠、輸電線路與核電廠延宕10至20年。豐盛無法等待。
2. 重複成本 競爭性的專有標準（充電接口、電池格式、作業系統生態）強迫重複投資。每家企業都在重新發明輪子——然後跟你收費。
3. 崩潰成本 沒有韌性機制。當供應鏈斷裂或氣候災難來襲時，市場沒有應變方案——只有對富人的紓困。
4. 信息不對稱 關鍵的能源與AI數據由私人壟斷者持有。公民、監管機構甚至政府都在黑暗中運作。
5. 心理成本 就業不穩定、零工經濟的脆弱性、債務驅動的焦慮——這些是結構性特徵
