After sovereign debt crises and multiple bailouts, Greece's governance structure remains trapped in high administrative rigidity, information opacity, and deep social psychological trauma. Value capture is concentrated among a few interest groups, while costs are broadly shifted to ordinary citizens and small-to-medium enterprises.

Key Findings

  • Administrative rigidity — Public sector digitalization is slow; waiting and repetition costs are extremely high, especially in the judiciary and land registration systems.

  • Social psychological trauma — A decade of austerity has created generational trauma, reflected in low political participation and widespread distrust of institutions.

Hallazgos Clave
