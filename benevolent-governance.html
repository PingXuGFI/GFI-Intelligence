仁政

從千年治理傳統到當代中國的減貧奇蹟

徐萍 著 · GFI Flow Intelligence

作者簡介

徐萍，GFI Flow Intelligence 創始人。出生於台灣，成年後赴美工作生活多年。在美期間，親歷資本主義社會高度發達的表象之下，國家政策被資本與地產階層深度操控的現實——土地增值被少數人捕獲、競選政治被巨額資金裹挾、社會福利淪為「管理窮人」的工具而非「消除貧困」的路徑。正是這種切身的觀察與思考，促使作者將研究視野投向全球不同制度下的社會治理比較。

2016年2月，作者親赴美國佛羅里達州維納斯基基地（The Venus Project）訪問，與創始人雅克·弗斯科（Jacque Fresco）合影交流。雅克·弗斯科終其一生，只有小學學歷。在主流精英社會眼中，他只是一個沒有正規教育背景的「怪人」——被大學拒絕、被媒體嘲笑、被學術界視為「業餘」。然而，正是這位「小學畢業的怪人」，花了數十年時間，設計出一套試圖徹底超越貨幣體系的「資源導向型經濟」（Resource-Based Economy），倡導以科學方法管理全球資源。他的思想被當時的美國主流社會視為過於超前、不切實際，甚至荒謬——但作者在他身上看到了一種被精英階級集體過濾掉的膽識：敢於質疑貨幣本身的正當性。維納斯計劃所倡導的「資源導向型經濟」——一種主張最終徹底超越貨幣體系、以科學方法管理全球資源的社會設計理念——在當時美國主流社會氛圍中被視為過於超前甚至不切實際。作者認同維納斯計劃對貨幣體系不可持續的診斷，但主張以「無條件基本收入」（Unconditional Basic Income, UBI）作為貨幣體系崩塌前的「軟著陸」方案，避免社會在轉型過程中陷入失序。

基於這一理念，作者撰寫了 《The Last Poverty Solution: A Global Commons Funding System for Universal Essential Access》（《最後的貧困解決方案：全球公域可持續基金與普遍基本接入》）。該書提出了一套以「全球公域貢獻機制」（GCCM）和「人道主義消費配額」（HCQ）為核心的雙軌架構——一個基於規則的、與使用量掛鉤的貢獻體系，從影響全球公域或跨界系統的活動中生成可預測的結構性資源，並將其轉化為可追蹤、可問責的人道主義消費權限，旨在為聯合國系統提供可預測、不侵犯主權、法律可依的21世紀融資機制。

篇首語

本書名為《仁政》。

「仁政」二字，源於中國最古老的政治傳統。兩千多年前，孟子對梁惠王說：「王如施仁政於民，省刑罰，薄稅斂，深耕易耨。」他告訴君王：真正的治理，不是壓榨，不是管控，是讓人民過上好日子。

四千年後，這個古老的理想在中國大地上變成了現實——9899萬農村貧困人口全部脫貧，8億多人擺脫貧困。這是人類歷史上規模最大、速度最快、成效最顯著的減貧行動。

這不是「奇蹟」。奇蹟是從天而降的、不可解釋的、無法複製的。而中國減貧的每一步，都清清楚楚、有跡可循——有制度、有方法、有人幹、有結果。

這是「仁政」。仁政不是恩賜，是本分；不是施捨，是責任；不是奇蹟，是制度必然。

與此同時，那些自封「民主燈塔」的西方國家，正在經歷深刻的制度危機。他們蓋不出地鐵、修不好公路、讓人民睡大街、讓兒童挨餓——卻還有臉把一套自己都不信的制度硬塞給中國。

本書要做的，就是兩件事：

  • 第一，講清楚中國做對了什麼。 從千年治理傳統的民本思想，到當代脫貧攻堅的系統實踐，中國走出了一條完全不同於西方的道路。這條道路根植於自己的文明，服務於自己的人，用實實在在的「交付」證明了自己的正當性。
  • 第二，戳穿西方硬塞給中國的謊言。 選舉不是民主的全部，自由不是資本的專利，治理不是表演的舞台。西方政府早已退化為資本地主的資產管理公司——他們沒有資格教別人怎麼治理。
「仁政」不是一本書的名字。它是中國治理的底色，是8億人命運改變的總稱，是人間正道。 —— 是為序

上篇 · 千年之根：中國治理傳統中的民本思想

一個連續五千年未中斷的文明，其治理思想必然是在長期的歷史實踐中累積形成的。中國治理傳統中的核心概念——如「民本」、「選賢」、「大一統」——並非源於抽象的理論建構，而是從大禹治水、都江堰、大運河、科舉制度、荒政賑災等具體治理實踐中逐步提煉而成的。

「民惟邦本，本固邦寧」——《尚書》中的這一表述，可以視為中國政治傳統中國家與人民關係的最早理論概括。它提出了一個核心命題：國家的正當性基礎在於人民的福祉，而非君主、精英或特定集團的意志。

第一章 從「民惟邦本」到「以人民為中心」

「民惟邦本，本固邦寧。」——《尚書·五子之歌》

（一）民本思想的經典表述

  • 《尚書》：「民惟邦本，本固邦寧。」將「人民」確立為國家的根本。
  • 孔子：「節用而愛人，使民以時。」要求節約資源、關注民生。
  • 老子：「治大國若烹小鮮。」強調尊重規律，避免過度干預。
  • 孟子：「王如施仁政於民，省刑罰，薄稅斂。」將「仁政」具體化；「民為貴，社稷次之，君為輕。」確立「民」為政治正當性的最終來源；「老吾老以及人之老，幼吾幼以及人之幼。」將家庭倫理擴展為社會互助。

（二）民本思想的歷史延續與當代轉化

從《尚書》到《孟子》，從「民惟邦本」到「以人民為中心」——當代中國脫貧攻堅的實踐，正是「民本」理念的制度轉化。其核心邏輯的一致性在於：將人民的基本生活保障視為國家治理的優先目標，並通過制度化的方式加以實現。

第二章 選賢與能——中國幹部選拔的制度基因

「為政以德，譬如北辰，居其所而眾星共之。」——《論語·為政》

（一）選拔機制的核心問題

任何政治體系都需要回答：以何種標準選拔治理者？中國給出的答案是「選賢與能」——正當性來源於能力與德行，而非出身、財富或言辭。

（二）科舉制度的歷史經驗

從漢代察舉制到隋唐科舉制，中國在兩千多年前便開始探索以考試選拔官員的制度化路徑。其特徵為：標準相對客觀、面向社會開放、內容與治理相關，在世界政治史上具有開創性意義。

（三）當代幹部選拔的制度特徵

當代中國的幹部選拔制度在理念上與「選賢與能」一脈相承，具體運作具有以下特徵：多層級歷練機制、實績導向的考核體系、組織考察與群眾評價相結合。

（四）選拔機制的比較視角

選舉制度傾向於獎勵「選舉能力」（演講、媒體應對、競選組織），而這些與治理所需的政策分析、行政管理、跨部門協調等能力並不必然重合。中國制度的設計邏輯是：以治理實績而非言辭能力為選拔依據，在邏輯上與「治理」本身具有更高的匹配度。

第三章 「大一統」與國家能力

「不謀萬世者，不足謀一時；不謀全局者，不足謀一域。」——陳澹然《寤言》

（一）「大一統」的歷史形成

公元前221年，秦統一六國，推行「書同文、車同軌、度同制」。中國由此成為世界上最早建立統一市場的國家之一。「大一統」不僅指領土統一，更指制度框架的統一，為大規模公共工程提供了必要條件。

（二）歷史案例：大型公共工程的組織能力

都江堰（始建於公元前256年）與大運河（始建於公元605年）是中國歷史上兩個代表性的大型公共工程。大型基礎設施具有投資大、週期長、受益廣的特徵，需要由國家作為主要組織者來推進。

（三）當代延續：基礎設施建設的制度基礎

成果：高鐵運營里程4.6萬公里、高速公路18萬公里、行政村100%通電、行政村通光纖率超98%。制度基礎包括政策連續性機制、跨區域協調能力、資源動員能力。

（四）比較分析

中國的「大一統」傳統在當代的制度轉化——統一的規劃體系、層級化的執行機制、持續的政策框架——構成了其在大型公共工程領域供給效能的制度基礎。

第四章 土地、資本與權力——西方治理結構的制度性分析

「民為貴，社稷次之，君為輕。」——《孟子·盡心下》

（一）英國：土地所有權的高度集中及其制度性成因

  • 現狀：僅佔人口約0.6%的世襲貴族和鄉紳階層，掌握了全國約三分之二的土地。英格蘭地區約一半的土地，由不足1%的人口持有。
  • 制度根源：英國上議院中保留的92個世襲貴族席位，意味著立法機構中仍有成員是依據出身而非選舉產生。
  • 關鍵機制：當農業用地被規劃為開發用地時，其市場價值可增長數十倍甚至上百倍——這一增值並非土地所有者的貢獻，而是社會發展和公共投資的結果，卻被私人完全捕獲。

（二）美國：競選資金、旋轉門與政策偏差

  • 競選資金：2024年大選總花費約159億美元，排名前1%的捐贈者提供了超過70%的「超級政治行動委員會」資金。
  • 旋轉門：政府高層職位與私營部門之間的人員流動是制度化常態。
  • 政策輸出：收入最低的50%家庭僅持有全國約2.5%的財富；約2600萬美國人缺乏基本醫保；年均槍擊死亡人數約4萬；基礎設施評級為C-。
英國模式：你出生在哪座莊園，決定了你一生的高度。
中國模式：你出生在哪座大山，國家修路架橋、建學校、搞扶貧，把你接出來。
誰在真正治理？誰在替地主管家？高下立判。

第五章 選舉政治的治理效能悖論

「巧言令色，鮮矣仁。」——《論語·學而》

選舉動員技能

公共演講與修辭、媒體關係、對手攻擊策略、選民情緒把握

公共治理技能

政策分析與決策、跨部門協調、長期戰略規劃、危機管理

以美國為例，總統任期四年，連任不超過兩屆。在有效施政時間不足一半的約束下，政策呈現「短期偏好」：基礎設施投資被推遲、氣候政策反覆搖擺、社會保障改革被擱置。中國以「五年規劃」為基本週期，自1953年以來已連續執行七十餘年，形成「一任接著一任幹」的政策連續性。

第六章 GDP核算的局限性

「名實相符，則治；名實不符，則亂。」——《管子·君臣上》

GDP的發明者西蒙·庫茲涅茨在1934年即已明確指出：「國民總產值不應被用來衡量經濟福利」。

  • 交易性質的「價值中立」：清理環境災難與建設學校在統計上等價。
  • 「產出」與「損耗」的失衡：自然資本損耗和基礎設施折舊未扣除。
  • 總量掩蓋分配：美國實際GDP增長，但中位數時薪增長率遠低於勞動生產率增速。
GDP是半套賬本，仁政才是全套答卷。
GDP可以告訴你一個國家「生產了多少」，但它從不告訴你——誰餓著肚子？誰睡在街頭？誰的孩子上不起學？

第七章 公共服務供給能力的國際比較

美國：全球最高GDP與基礎設施長期失修

  • 美國土木工程師學會2021年評估結果為C-：43%的公共道路狀況「差」或「一般」，超過4.6萬座橋樑結構有缺陷。
  • 成因：選舉週期、利益集團、政府核心能力空心化、政黨極化。

英國：全民醫療體系的供給危機

  • NHS候診名單超過750萬人，因候診延誤導致額外死亡人數每年達數千至上萬人。
  • 政府日益依賴外部諮詢公司，內部專業能力持續流失。

中國公共服務供給的制度邏輯

  • 成效：高鐵4.6萬公里、高速公路18萬公里、行政村100%通電。
  • 制度基礎：政策連續性、政府核心能力、財政資源導向配置、動員與執行機制。

第八章 福利制度的設計困境

「老吾老以及人之老，幼吾幼以及人之幼。」——《孟子·梁惠王上》

福利制度存在兩條根本性的分界線：「管理貧困」與「消除貧困」。

  • 美國福利懸崖：收入小幅增加可能導致福利大幅喪失。SNAP項目的不當支付率約10.62%，不當支付總額約101億美元。
  • 英國懲罰性救助：國際特赦組織2025年報告指出，英國社會保障制度是「通過其設立的程序來懲罰、傷害和非人化人民的制度」。
  • 中國低保與精準扶貧：從「被動輸血」轉向「主動造血」。低保制度覆蓋曾超過7000萬城鄉貧困人口，設計特徵包括家庭收入認定、分類施保、動態管理、漸進式退出。
西方的福利制度，是用金錢把窮人關進籠子。
中國的扶貧事業，是用發展把窮人領出大山。

第九章 複雜治理中的系統思維

「不謀萬世者，不足謀一時；不謀全局者，不足謀一域。」——陳澹然《寤言》
  • 線性思維的代價：阿富汗安全部隊建設（830億美元，11天崩潰）；越戰策略套用（58,220名美軍陣亡）；德國病假政策（限制病假導致病情加重）。
  • 脫貧攻堅的系統思維：問題識別、干預設計、多層級執行、動態監測、五年過渡期。
西方用線性思維治理複雜系統——花830億美元訓練阿富汗軍隊，11天崩塌。
中國用系統思維治理複雜系統——花40年讓8億人脫貧。

Part I · Millennia of Governance — The People-Centered Tradition in Chinese Statecraft

A civilization uninterrupted for five thousand years necessarily accumulates its governing wisdom through long historical practice. Core concepts in China's governing tradition — such as "people-centered," "selecting the virtuous and capable," and "great unity" — did not arise from abstract theoretical construction but were gradually distilled from concrete governing practices: Yu the Great taming the floods, the Dujiangyan Irrigation System, the Grand Canal, the imperial examination system, and famine relief.

"The people are the foundation of the state; when the foundation is firm, the state is at peace." This statement from the Book of Documents can be regarded as the earliest theoretical formulation in China's political tradition concerning the relationship between state and people. It proposes a core proposition: the legitimacy of the state rests on the well-being of the people, not on the will of the sovereign, elites, or any particular group.

Chapter 1: From "The People Are the Foundation of the State" to "People-Centered"

"The people are the foundation of the state; when the foundation is firm, the state is at peace." — Book of Documents, "Songs of the Five Sons"

(I) The Classical Formulation of People-Centered Thought

  • Book of Documents: "The people are the foundation of the state; when the foundation is firm, the state is at peace." Establishes "the people" as the foundation of the state.
  • Confucius: "Practice frugality and love the people; employ the people in accordance with the seasons." Calls for resource conservation and concern for people's livelihoods.
  • Laozi: "Governing a great state is like cooking a small fish." Emphasizes respecting natural laws and avoiding excessive intervention.
  • Mencius: "If the king practices benevolent governance for the people, reducing punishments and taxes." Concretizes "benevolent governance"; "The people are the most important, the state comes next, and the sovereign is the least." Establishes "the people" as the ultimate source of political legitimacy; "Extend respect for the elderly in your own family to the elderly in others' families; extend care for the young in your own family to the young in others' families." Extends family ethics to social mutual assistance.

(II) Historical Continuity and Contemporary Transformation of People-Centered Thought

From the Book of Documents to Mencius, from "the people are the foundation of the state" to "people-centered" — contemporary China's poverty alleviation practice is precisely the institutional transformation of the "people-centered" philosophy. The consistency of its core logic lies in: treating the basic livelihood security of the people as the priority of national governance and realizing it through institutionalized means.

Chapter 2: Selecting the Virtuous and Capable — The Institutional Gene of China's Cadre Selection

"Governing with virtue is like the North Star: it remains in its place while all the other stars orbit around it." — The Analects, "Wei Zheng"

(I) The Core Question of Selection Mechanisms

Every political system must answer a fundamental question: By what criteria are rulers selected? China's answer is "selecting the virtuous and capable" — legitimacy derives from ability and moral character, not from birth, wealth, or rhetorical skills.

(II) The Historical Experience of the Imperial Examination System

From the Han dynasty's recommendation system to the Sui and Tang dynasties' imperial examination system, China began exploring institutionalized paths for selecting officials through examination more than two thousand years ago. The imperial examination system constituted the main channel for official selection in China for many centuries. Its basic features were: relatively objective standards, openness to all social strata, and content relevant to governance. As a large-scale, standardized talent selection mechanism, it was pioneering in world political history.

(III) Institutional Features of Contemporary Cadre Selection

Contemporary China's cadre selection system maintains continuity with the "selecting the virtuous and capable" tradition at the conceptual level. In concrete operation, it features: multi-tiered training mechanisms, performance-oriented assessment systems, and a combination of organizational review and public evaluation.

(IV) A Comparative Perspective on Selection Mechanisms

Electoral systems tend to reward "electoral capacity" — including public speaking, media management, and campaign organization — yet these skills do not necessarily overlap with the policy analysis, administrative management, and cross-sectoral coordination required for governance. The design logic of contemporary China's cadre selection system is: selecting based on governing performance rather than rhetorical ability — logically more congruent with the function of "governance" itself.

Chapter 3: "Great Unity" and State Capacity

"One who does not plan for ten thousand generations is insufficient to plan for a single moment; one who does not plan for the whole situation is insufficient to plan for a single part." — Chen Danran, Wu Yan

(I) The Historical Formation of "Great Unity"

In 221 BCE, Qin unified the six states and implemented "uniform writing, uniform wheel tracks, and uniform weights and measures." These measures, on the basis of political unification, further realized institutional unification, market unification, and cultural unification. China thereby became one of the earliest countries in the world to establish a unified large market.

(II) Historical Cases: Organizational Capacity for Large-Scale Public Works

The Dujiangyan Irrigation System (begun in 256 BCE) and the Grand Canal (begun in 605 CE) are two representative large-scale public works in Chinese history. From the perspective of public economics, large-scale infrastructure is characterized by large investment, long construction periods, and broad benefits, and typically requires the state to act as the primary organizer.

(III) Contemporary Continuity: The Institutional Basis of Infrastructure Construction

Achievements include: 46,000 km of high-speed rail, 180,000 km of expressways, 100% electrification of administrative villages, and over 98% fiber-optic coverage of administrative villages. The institutional basis includes policy continuity mechanisms, cross-regional coordination capacity, and resource mobilization capacity.

(IV) Comparative Analysis

The contemporary institutional transformation of China's "great unity" tradition — including unified planning systems, tiered implementation mechanisms, and sustained policy frameworks — constitutes the institutional foundation for its effectiveness in large-scale public works provision.

Chapter 6: The Limitations of GDP Accounting and Their Policy Implications

"When name and reality correspond, there is order; when name and reality do not correspond, there is disorder." — Guanzi, "Jun Chen Shang"

GDP's inventor, Simon Kuznets, clearly pointed out its fundamental limitation when presenting his report to the U.S. Congress in 1934: "the welfare of a nation can scarcely be inferred from a measure of national income."

  • Aggregate figures mask distribution: U.S. real GDP has grown several times over, but inflation-adjusted median hourly wage growth has been far below labor productivity growth.

Afterword

Throughout the writing of this book, one core question has persisted: How does one evaluate the quality of a governance system?

One answer focuses on procedures — whether the government is produced through elections and whether there is multi-party competition. Another answer focuses on outcomes — whether the government has effectively provided security, well-being, infrastructure, and fair opportunities for development.

The position taken by this book is: The evaluation of governance systems should attend to both procedures and outcomes, but in the final analysis, the purpose of governance is the well-being of the people, not the self-operation of procedures.

China's governing tradition has provided a value system centered on "people-centeredness." The contemporary institutional transformation of this system has formed a performance-oriented cadre selection mechanism, a long-term planning policy framework, and a public goods provision model supported by large-scale resource mobilization.

The poverty alleviation practice presented in this book is an application case of this governance logic in a specific policy domain. It is not a closed "success story" but an ongoing institutional evolutionary process. The goal toward which it points — enabling the people to live good lives — is a proposition that all governance systems should jointly pursue.

We call this governance concept "benevolent governance."
It is not an ancient ideal; it is the daily reality of contemporary China.

II. Key Theses

  1. People-centered thought: "The people are the foundation of the state; when the foundation is firm, the state is at peace." — The core concept in China's political tradition of regarding people's well-being as the basis of state legitimacy has received institutional continuity in contemporary poverty alleviation practice.
  2. Selecting the virtuous and capable: China's cadre selection system is conceptually based on "selecting the virtuous and capable," screening governance talent through multi-tiered training and long-term performance observation — a design logic highly congruent with the function of "governance" itself.
  3. Great unity: The "great unity" tradition has been transformed in contemporary China into policy continuity mechanisms and cross-regional coordination capacity, providing the institutional foundation for large-scale public works and poverty reduction.
  4. Western democratic dilemma: The Western "democratic" model faces persistent performance challenges in land distribution, capital's influence on policy, and social mobility — the legitimacy of its claim to be a "universal template" should be prudently examined.
  5. Mismatch between elections and governance: Electoral systems tend to reward "electoral capacity" rather than "governing capacity" — a structural feature that creates tension with governance effectiveness.
  6. GDP limitations: GDP as an economic indicator has inherent limitations — it does not distinguish types of transactions, deduct resource depletion, or ask about wealth distribution — and should not be used as a sole measure of human well-being.
  7. Public service effectiveness: The effectiveness of public service delivery depends on the maintenance of government core capacity. When governments increasingly rely on external consultants, their autonomous planning and implementation capacity may face structural erosion.
  8. Welfare design paths: Welfare systems have two design paths — a punitive model oriented toward "managing poverty" and a developmental model oriented toward "eliminating poverty." The policy effectiveness of the two paths differs significantly.
  9. Systems thinking: The object of public policy is a complex adaptive system; linear thinking in such systems often produces unintended consequences. Systems thinking is a necessary methodological foundation for complex governance.
  10. Governance evaluation: The evaluation of governance systems should attend to both procedures and outcomes. The ultimate purpose of governance is the well-being of the people, not the self-operation of procedures.

III. On the GL Framework

The author, Xu Ping, based on her long-term accumulation in international policy research, has designed the GL Framework (Governance Legitimacy Framework) — a diagnostic tool for detecting "governance friction."

The GL Framework quantifies the governance legitimacy of 100 global economies through eight parameters (waiting cost, repetition cost, collapse cost, information asymmetry cost, psychological cost, opportunity cost, transfer pathways, and degree of closed-loop). Its core concept is: a government that systematically transfers administrative costs to the most vulnerable groups, no matter how procedurally compliant, has already had its legitimacy undermined.

The GL Framework is highly consistent with the core argument of Benevolent Governance — the former provides a quantitative diagnostic tool, while the latter unfolds historical and institutional analysis. Together they form a complete system from theory to methodology.
