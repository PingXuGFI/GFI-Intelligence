Who Truly Enjoyed the Prosperity?

The Silent Ledger of Land, Infrastructure, and Institutions
Ping Xu · Boston · GFI Flow Intelligence · June 2026
GL Framework asks: “Did the people actually receive the service?”
Not “was the transaction completed?” Not “was the process followed?” — but whether public investment actually reached the citizen as usable, timely Access.

1. Six Institutions, Six Cost‑Shift Pathways

🇬🇧 United Kingdom
Concentrated land holding + planning permission
Aristocracy, Crown, developers
truly enjoyed prosperity
Renters, taxpayers
bore the cost
Ghost GDP: waiting tax · rental tax · anxiety tax
Planning permission turns £23k/ha farmland into £2.67M/ha — a public decision, privately captured. 50% of England owned by <1%.
🇺🇸 United States
Market mechanisms + capital gains tax preference
Capital, REITs
truly enjoyed prosperity
Latecomer generations
bore the cost
Ghost GDP: commuting tax · anxiety tax
Homestead Act (1862) dispersed land, but interstate highways and REITs reconcentrated value. Land financialization turned housing into a speculative asset.
🇭🇰 Hong Kong
Leasehold system + rail‑plus‑property
Government + 4 developers
truly enjoyed prosperity
Young renters
bore the cost
Ghost GDP: 5.5‑year waiting tax · rental tax
MTR captures some increment, but 400 sq ft apartment consumes 50‑70% of a young professional’s salary. Public housing wait >5 years.
🇹🇼 Taiwan
Private ownership + announced land value
Landlords, developers
truly enjoyed prosperity
Younger generation
bore the cost
Ghost GDP: childbirth tax · commuting tax
“Increment returns to the public” is in the Constitution. Announced land value is 50‑70% below market price. Effective capture rate ~5%.
🇨🇳 China
State land + land finance
Public system (reinvested)
truly enjoyed prosperity
Local govt, land‑lost farmers
bore the cost
Ghost GDP: privacy tax · maturity mismatch
Land finance built 45,000 km HSR in 20 years. Maturity mismatch and tech‑driven friction elimination carry unmeasured costs.
🌍 Global Commons
First‑come, no effective governance
First arrivers, those with capacity
truly enjoyed prosperity
All humanity
bore the cost
Ghost GDP: ecological tax · future tax
High seas, Antarctica, deep‑sea minerals, outer space — “common heritage of all humanity” in law, but whoever has capacity gets the value.

2. The Prosperity Table — Who Truly Enjoyed It (by Institutional Logic)

🇬🇧 UK
Aristocracy, Crown, developers
Concentrated land holding — prosperity automatically flows to existing landowners since 1066.
🇺🇸 US
Capital (developers, REITs)
Market mechanisms + capital gains tax preference. Prosperity captured before infrastructure is announced.
🇭🇰 Hong Kong
Government + 4 developers
Land revenue + property development profits. Government auctions land; developers capture residual value.
🇹🇼 Taiwan
Landlords + developers
Announced land value artificially low, insufficient tax capture. Most prosperity stays private.
🇨🇳 China
Public system (reinvested)
Land concession fees fund next round of infrastructure. Closed loop, but dependent on rising asset prices.
🌍 Global Commons
First arrivers, those with capacity
No effective international management mechanism. Whoever can extract, takes.

3. The Ghost GDP Table — Who Bore the Unrecorded Cost

🇬🇧 UK
Waiting tax · rental tax · anxiety tax
Renters pay inflated rents while waiting years for public housing. Inefficient transport systems.
🇺🇸 US
Commuting tax · anxiety tax
Car‑dependent infrastructure forces low‑income families into high transport costs.
🇭🇰 Hong Kong
5.5‑year waiting tax · rental tax
Young professionals spend 50‑70% of income on rent. Public housing wait exceeds half a decade.
🇹🇼 Taiwan
Childbirth tax · commuting tax
Fertility rate 0.87. Young couples delay children because housing, commuting, childcare costs are prohibitive.
🇨🇳 China
Privacy tax · maturity mismatch
Technology eliminates friction but extracts data. Local government debt from land‑financed infrastructure.
🌍 Global Commons
Ecological tax · future tax
Overfishing, deep‑sea mining, space resource extraction — costs born by all humanity, benefits taken by first movers.

4. GL Framework — The Denominator That Was Never Measured

GL = (Fs × Vn) / (Pd × Cf)
Fs = Flow success rate — proportion of budget reaching citizens as usable Access.
Vn = Strategic value (1.5 = max).
Pd = Pain duration — waiting, redundancy, uncertainty.
Cf = Cognitive friction — complexity, info asymmetry, officer discretion.

A low GL means the system shifts cost outward instead of solving friction. Ghost GDP is that shifted cost — never recorded in any national ledger. A blind spot in how GDP accounts for economic activity.

“GDP asks: Was the transaction completed?”
“Traditional auditing asks: Was the process followed?”
“GL Framework asks: Did the people actually receive the service?”

Who truly enjoyed the prosperity?

¿Quién disfrutó verdaderamente la prosperidad?

El libro mayor silencioso de la tierra, la infraestructura y las instituciones
Ping Xu · Boston · GFI Flow Intelligence · Junio 2026
GL Framework pregunta: «¿Realmente la gente recibió el servicio?»
No «¿se completó la transacción?» ni «¿se siguió el proceso?» — sino si la inversión pública realmente llegó al ciudadano como Acceso (Access) usable y oportuno.

1. Seis instituciones, seis vías de transferencia de costos

🇬🇧 Reino Unido
Tierra concentrada + permiso de planificación
Aristocracia, Corona, promotores
disfrutaron la prosperidad
Inquilinos, contribuyentes
soportaron el costo
Ghost GDP: impuesto de espera · impuesto de alquiler · impuesto de ansiedad
El permiso de planificación convierte tierra agrícola de £23k/ha en £2.67M/ha — una decisión pública, capturada privadamente. 50% de Inglaterra propiedad de <1%.
🇺🇸 Estados Unidos
Mercado + preferencia fiscal a ganancias de capital
Capital, REITs
disfrutaron la prosperidad
Generaciones tardías
soportaron costo
Ghost GDP: impuesto de commuting · impuesto de ansiedad
La Homestead Act (1862) dispersó la tierra, pero las interestatales y los REITs reconcentraron el valor. La financiarización del suelo convirtió la vivienda en activo especulativo.
🇭🇰 Hong Kong
Enfiteusis + ferrocarril‑más‑propiedad
Gobierno + 4 grandes promotores
disfrutaron la prosperidad
Jóvenes inquilinos
soportaron costo
Ghost GDP: impuesto de espera 5.5 años · impuesto de alquiler
MTR captura parte del incremento, pero un piso de 400 pies² consume 50‑70% del salario de un joven profesional. Espera de vivienda pública >5 años.
🇹🇼 Taiwán
Propiedad privada + valor de suelo declarado
Terratenientes, promotores
disfrutaron la prosperidad
Generación joven
soportó costo
Ghost GDP: impuesto de natalidad · impuesto de commuting
«El incremento vuelve al público» está en la Constitución. El valor de suelo declarado está 50‑70% por debajo del mercado. Captura efectiva ~5%.
🇨🇳 China
Tierra estatal + financiación del suelo
Sistema público (reinvertido)
disfrutó la prosperidad
Gobierno local, campesinos sin tierra
soportaron costo
Ghost GDP: impuesto de privacidad · descalce de plazos
La financiación del suelo construyó 45.000 km de AVE en 20 años. El descalce de plazos y la eliminación tecnológica de fricciones tienen costos no medidos.
🌍 Bienes Comunes Globales
Quien llega primero, sin gobernanza efectiva
Primeros en llegar, los que tienen capacidad
disfrutaron la prosperidad
Toda la humanidad
soportó costo
Ghost GDP: impuesto ecológico · impuesto futuro
Alta mar, Antártida, minerales de aguas profundas, espacio exterior — «patrimonio común de la humanidad» en la ley, pero quien tiene capacidad se lleva el valor.
