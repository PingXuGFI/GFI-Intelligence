What GL Measures

GL = (Fs × Vn) / (Pd × Cf)
Fs = Flow success rate — value delivered vs. friction created.
Vn = Strategic value — necessity of the service.
Pd = Pain duration — waiting, uncertainty, redundancy.
Cf = Cognitive friction — complexity, information asymmetry, hidden rules.
“GDP asks: Was the transaction completed?”
“Traditional auditing asks: Was the process followed?”
“GL Framework asks: Did the citizen receive value for every dollar paid?”
