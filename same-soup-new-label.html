Same Soup, New Label

From Colony to 'Democracy' — the Landlord Never Changes
GFI · Critique of the West Series

The British Empire's "indirect rule" manual has become the global "democracy promotion" playbook. The labels have changed — from "civilizing mission" to "democracy promotion" — but the structure remains the same: a small elite extracts wealth, controls politics, and leaves the majority powerless.

I. The Colonial Template: Indirect Rule

The British Empire perfected a governance model called "indirect rule." Instead of direct administration, it co-opted local elites — chiefs, kings, landlords — and empowered them to collect taxes, enforce order, and maintain the status quo. In exchange, these elites received British protection, wealth, and legitimacy. The majority of the population remained excluded, exploited, and governed without consent.

The structure was simple: external power + local elite = control. The British didn't need to occupy every village; they just needed to control the elites who controlled the villages.

II. The Modern Template: Democracy Promotion

After decolonization, the template was updated. "Indirect rule" became "democracy promotion." Local elites became "democratic leaders." The extraction mechanism became "free markets" and "structural adjustment." The legitimacy language became "elections" and "civil society."

But the structure remained the same: external power + local elite = control. The U.S. and its allies don't need to occupy every country; they just need to control the elites who control the countries — through funding, training, media, and conditional aid.

V. Conclusion: The Label Is Not the Structure

"Democracy" is not a system of popular control. It is a legitimacy label — applied to a structure that continues the colonial pattern of extraction and elite rule. The people remain excluded. The landlord remains in place.

To see the structure, ignore the labels. Look at who governs, who benefits, and who pays the cost. The answer has not changed in 200 years.

同樣的湯，新標籤

從殖民地到「民主」，地主從未改變
GFI · 美國批判系列

大英帝國的「間接統治」手冊已成為全球「民主推廣」劇本。標籤變了——從「文明使命」到「民主推廣」——但結構不變：少數菁英提取財富、控制政治、讓多數人無能為力。

一、殖民模板：間接統治

大英帝國完善了一種稱為 「間接統治」 的治理模式。它不直接管理，而是吸納地方菁英——酋長、國王、地主——並授權他們徵稅、維持秩序、維持現狀。作為交換，這些菁英獲得英國的保護、財富和正當性。大多數人口仍然被排除在外、被剝削、在未經同意的情況下被治理。

結構很簡單：外部權力 + 地方菁英 = 控制。英國不需要佔領每個村莊；只需要控制那些控制村莊的菁英。

二、現代模板：民主推廣

非殖民化後，這個模板被更新了。「間接統治」變成了 「民主推廣」。地方菁英變成了「民主領袖」。提取機制變成了「自由市場」和「結構調整」。正當性語言變成了「選舉」和「公民社會」。

但結構不變：外部權力 + 地方菁英 = 控制。美國及其盟友不需要佔領每個國家；只需要透過資助、培訓、媒體和有條件的援助來控制那些控制國家的菁英。

三、連續性：地主從未改變

維度殖民時代現代「民主」
外部權力大英帝國美國 / IMF / 世界銀行 / NATO
地方夥伴酋長 · 國王 · 地主民選官員 · 寡頭 · 媒體所有者

結果❌ 菁英致富，大眾貧窮❌ 菁英致富，大眾貧窮

標籤變了，但結構完全相同。地主——控制系統的外部權力——從未改變。

五、結論：標籤不是結構

「民主」不是人民控制的系統。它是一個正當性標籤——貼在延續殖民掠奪與菁英統治模式的結構上。人民仍然被排除在外。地主仍然在位。

要看結構，忽略標籤。看誰在統治、誰在受益、誰在付出代價。答案在 200 年來從未改變。
