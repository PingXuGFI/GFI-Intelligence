Ghana scores 0.36 on the GL Index — a D grade. As a resource-rich country in West Africa, Ghana has gold, cocoa, and oil resources. However, resource dependency, institutional fragility, and resource revenues captured by a few groups constitute major gaps in governance legitimacy. Waiting and repetition costs are high, and ordinary people continuously lose opportunities and quality of life through resource exploitation and institutional friction.
