Key Findings

  • Digital Public Infrastructure — UPI (unified payments) and Aadhaar (digital identity) are world-leading. Digital governance works — but only for those with access.
  • Administrative Friction — Bureaucracy is fragmented and slow. Corruption is endemic.
  • Inequality — The gap between the digital elite and the poor is enormous. Access is not equal.
  • Federalism — States have significant autonomy, but this creates fragmentation and inconsistency.
  • Infrastructure — Roads, energy, and water are underdeveloped. Opportunity costs are high.
  • Social Trust — Trust in institutions is low. The system is often seen as working for the powerful, not for the many.

What 0.20 Means

India is a country of extremes. Its digital infrastructure is world-class. Its administrative friction is among the highest in the world. The system delivers for the digital elite — but leaves the majority behind. The gap between potential and execution is enormous. India has the resources to do better. It lacks the institutional capacity to deliver for all.
