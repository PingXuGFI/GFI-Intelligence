Kazakhstan scores 0.47 on the GL Index — a D grade. As the largest economy in Central Asia, Kazakhstan has abundant energy and mineral resources. However, an oversized bureaucracy, low administrative efficiency, and resource wealth captured by a few groups constitute major gaps in governance legitimacy. Waiting and repetition costs are very high, and ordinary people continuously lose time and opportunity through institutional friction.
