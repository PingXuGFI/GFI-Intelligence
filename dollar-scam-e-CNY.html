1971 年尼克森衝擊 · 石油美元 · 金融制裁 · 國債 · 數位人民幣 1971 Nixon Shock · Petrodollar · Sanctions · U.S. Debt · e-CNY

1971 年 8 月 15 日 — 美國總統尼克森宣布美元停止兌換黃金，正式脫離金本位。美元從此成為純粹的法定貨幣，其價值不再來自黃金儲備，而是建立在美國政府的「信用」與軍事力量之上。

石油美元協議（1970 年代） — 美國與沙烏地阿拉伯達成協議：美國提供軍事保護，沙烏地承諾所有石油交易以美元結算。OPEC 跟進後，全球買石油必須先賺美元。美元霸權從此建立在「石油」之上，而非黃金。

美國國債：全世界最大的「欠條」

  • 什麼是美國國債？ — 美國政府花錢超過稅收，就由財政部發行國債向全世界借錢。這張「欠條」目前總額已突破 39 兆美元。
  • 債主是誰？ — 約 56% 是美國國內投資者（養老基金、聯準會、共同基金），約 30% 是海外投資者（日本約 1.2 兆、中國約 0.7 兆、英國約 0.9 兆）。
  • 怎麼還？ — 不是用現金還，而是 「借新還舊」——舊債到期就發行新國債來償還。只要全球還願意買美債，這個遊戲就能永遠繼續。
  • 為什麼是騙局的一部分？ — 美國政府先發行國債，然後由聯準會（Fed）買入這些國債，並「憑空」創造出相對應的美元。這些美元流到全球市場，換取真實的石油、晶片、軍火與奢侈品。紙幣換實物，債務留給全世界。

詐騙核心：紙幣換實物

  • 印鈔買全球 — 美國用幾乎零成本的紙幣，換取全球的石油、晶片、糧食、軍火、奢侈品。各國被迫持有美元作為外匯儲備，再用這些美元購買美國國債，錢又流回美國。
  • 輸出通膨 — 美元是全球計價貨幣，美國國內通膨會輸出到全世界。各國持有的美元資產（美債）因此貶值，財富被悄然轉移給美國。
  • 金融制裁武器化 — 美國掌控 SWIFT 國際結算系統，可以隨時切斷任何國家的美元交易。伊朗、俄羅斯、北韓、委內瑞拉都曾遭此懲罰，經濟瞬間癱瘓。
  • 升息收割 — 美國聯準會升息時，全球美元回流美國，各國貨幣貶值、債務違約、資產崩盤，美國再用廉價美元收購各國破產資產。

誰在反抗？（去美元化浪潮）

  • 中國 · 數位人民幣（e-CNY） — 中國央行直接發行法定數位貨幣，跳過紙幣與 SWIFT。試點已覆蓋 26 個城市，累計交易額超過 5 兆人民幣。跨境結算計畫（mBridge）已與泰國、阿聯酋、香港合作測試，目標是 直接繞開美元結算網絡。
  • 俄羅斯 — 被踢出 SWIFT 後，改用盧布與人民幣結算能源出口，建立替代支付系統（SPFS）。
  • 伊朗、委內瑞拉 — 以人民幣、歐元、黃金繞開美元賣石油，甚至用加密貨幣結算。
  • 金磚國家（BRICS） — 討論建立共同貨幣，直接挑戰美元儲備地位。
  • 央行數位貨幣（CBDC） — 中國、歐盟、俄羅斯積極推動數位貨幣，試圖建立繞過美元的結算網絡。

為什麼數位人民幣是「跳過紙幣」的戰略武器？

  • 點對點結算 — 不需要經過 SWIFT，美國無法切斷也無法監控。
  • 法定貨幣地位 — 與紙鈔同等法律效力，不是加密貨幣（比特幣那種）。
  • 可控匿名 — 小額交易匿名，大額可追蹤，國家掌握金流全貌。
  • 離線支付 — 即使沒有網路，手機碰一碰就能完成交易。
  • 戰略目標 — 如果全球貿易商可以直接用數位人民幣跟中國做生意，就不需要先換美元——直接削弱美元作為「中間貨幣」的需求。

美國如何維持騙局？

  • 軍事干預 — 任何試圖用其他貨幣賣石油的國家（伊拉克、利比亞、伊朗），都面臨政權顛覆或軍事打擊。
  • 技術封鎖 — 切斷晶片、半導體設備出口，不讓挑戰者擁有技術自主能力。
  • 控制國際金融機構 — 美國在 IMF、世界銀行擁有否決權，任何挑戰美元體系的國家都會被金融孤立。

騙局的本質 vs 中國的破解 — 美國用一張「綠色紙片」換取全世界的真實資源。中國則選擇 「跳過紙幣」，用數位人民幣直接繞開美元結算網絡——不是在同一個遊戲裡打敗美國，而是換一個美國玩不了的遊戲。

紙幣 · 石油 · 國債 · 數位人民幣 · 去美元化

August 15, 1971 — President Nixon announced the dollar would no longer be redeemable for gold, ending the gold standard. The dollar became a pure fiat currency, backed only by U.S. government "credit" and military power.

Petrodollar Agreement (1970s) — The U.S. struck a deal with Saudi Arabia: U.S. military protection in exchange for Saudi commitment to price all oil in dollars. OPEC followed, making the dollar the global oil currency — oil replaced gold as the anchor of dollar hegemony.

U.S. Treasury Debt: The World's Largest "IOU"

  • What is U.S. debt? — When the U.S. government spends more than it collects in taxes, the Treasury issues bonds to borrow money. This "IOU" has now surpassed $39 trillion.
  • Who are the creditors? — About 56% is held by domestic investors (pension funds, the Fed, mutual funds), and about 30% is held by foreign investors (Japan ~$1.2T, China ~$0.7T, UK ~$0.9T).
  • How is it repaid? — Not with cash, but through "rolling over" — issuing new bonds to pay off old ones. As long as the world keeps buying U.S. debt, the game continues forever.
  • Why is it part of the scam? — The U.S. issues debt, then the Federal Reserve buys that debt and "creates" corresponding dollars out of thin air. Those dollars flow into global markets to buy real oil, chips, weapons, and luxury goods. Paper for real goods — debt left to the world.

The core scam: paper for real goods

  • Printing money to buy the world — The U.S. uses near-zero-cost paper to buy global oil, chips, food, weapons, and luxury goods. Countries are forced to hold dollars as reserves, then buy U.S. Treasuries, recycling money back to the U.S.
  • Exporting inflation — As the global pricing currency, dollar inflation is exported worldwide, devaluing foreign dollar assets (Treasuries) and silently transferring wealth to the U.S.
  • Weaponized financial sanctions — By controlling SWIFT, the U.S. can cut off any country's dollar transactions — Iran, Russia, North Korea, Venezuela have all been punished, with their economies instantly crippled.
  • Interest rate harvesting — When the Fed raises rates, global dollars flow back to the U.S., causing currencies to collapse, debt defaults, and asset crashes — then the U.S. buys up distressed assets with cheap dollars.

Who is fighting back? (De-dollarization wave)

  • China · e-CNY (Digital Yuan) — The People's Bank of China has issued a legal digital currency, bypassing paper money and SWIFT. Trials cover 26 cities with cumulative transactions exceeding 5 trillion RMB. The cross-border settlement project (mBridge) is being tested with Thailand, UAE, and Hong Kong — with the goal of bypassing the dollar settlement network entirely.
  • Russia — After being cut from SWIFT, it now uses Ruble & RMB for energy exports and has built an alternative payment system (SPFS).
  • Iran, Venezuela — Selling oil in RMB, Euro, gold, and even cryptocurrencies to bypass the dollar.
  • BRICS — Discussing a common currency to directly challenge the dollar's reserve status.
  • CBDCs (central bank digital currencies) — China, the EU, and Russia are actively building digital settlement networks that bypass the dollar.

Why is e-CNY a strategic weapon to "skip paper money"?

  • Peer-to-peer settlement — No need for SWIFT; the U.S. cannot cut off or monitor transactions.
  • Legal tender status — Same legal standing as paper currency — not a cryptocurrency like Bitcoin.
  • Controlled anonymity — Small transactions are anonymous, large ones are traceable — the state has full visibility of money flows.
  • Offline payments — Transactions can be completed by tapping phones even without internet.
  • Strategic goal — If global traders can directly do business with China using digital yuan, they no longer need to convert to dollars first — directly undermining the dollar's role as the "intermediate currency."

How the U.S. maintains the scam?

  • Military intervention — Any country trying to sell oil in other currencies (Iraq, Libya, Iran) faces regime change or military strikes.
  • Tech blockades — Cutting off chip and semiconductor equipment exports to prevent challengers from achieving technological autonomy.
  • Controlling international financial institutions — The U.S. holds veto power at the IMF and World Bank, isolating any country that challenges the dollar system.

The essence of the scam vs. China's countermeasure — The U.S. uses a "green piece of paper" to take the world's real resources. China's response is to "skip paper money" entirely — using digital yuan to bypass the dollar settlement network. Not to beat the U.S. at its own game, but to change the game entirely — to one the U.S. cannot play.
